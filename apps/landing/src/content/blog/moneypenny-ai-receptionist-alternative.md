## LobbyStack preserves budget when you already have people

Consider 100 calls that average three minutes. Moneypenny's published 100-call plan costs $199. LobbyStack Pro covers the resulting 300 minutes inside its $100 allowance and leaves another 200 minutes available.

The comparison changes if your company needs Moneypenny's people to take escalations. Their labor forms part of the product. A business with an owner, dispatcher, or on-call employee already available does not need to pay for an external receptionist network.

LobbyStack routes the sensitive call to that employee with context and keeps the transcript and summary. The company saves on software, controls the escalation rules, and can use managed cloud or its own infrastructure.

## Moneypenny sells operational support

Moneypenny built its name around human answering. Its AI receptionist adds qualification, scheduling, routing, FAQ answers, data collection, voice customization, analytics, transcripts, and SMS follow-up. Calls can escalate to the customer's team or to Moneypenny staff.

That managed model can help a business without employees available for transfers. It also gives buyers one vendor for AI coverage and live receptionist service.

LobbyStack transfers calls to people the business configures. It sends context through caller details, summaries, transcripts, and notifications, but it does not include an external receptionist workforce. A company can connect another answering partner, though it owns that relationship.

## Per-call prices reflect the service model

Moneypenny's public US plans list:

- $69 for 25 calls
- $99 for 50 calls
- $199 for 100 calls
- $435 for 250 calls
- $775 for 500 calls

Published overages range from $2.49 to $1.69 per call.

LobbyStack lists 150 voice minutes for $30 and 500 for $100. The unit is different, and LobbyStack does not include Moneypenny's live-agent network.

A five-minute call consumes one Moneypenny call allowance and five LobbyStack minutes. Ten 30-second calls consume ten Moneypenny call allowances and five LobbyStack minutes. Use the business's duration and escalation rate to calculate the full cost.

## Managed onboarding or inspectable deployment

Moneypenny emphasizes managed onboarding and service. That can reduce setup risk for an owner who wants someone to shape the receptionist and remain available when calls change.

LobbyStack's cloud is self-serve, with Enterprise support for more complex needs. The application code is public, and a technical team can self-host. It can choose providers, configure retention, inspect call behavior, and adapt the product.

The Moneypenny pages reviewed for this article do not advertise self-hosting or public source access. The absence of those options may not matter to a small business. It matters to agencies, regulated teams, and buyers who require an exit path from a hosted platform.

## A funeral-home example

A funeral home receives routine service questions and calls from people in distress. It wants 24-hour coverage, but the staff cannot take every escalation overnight.

Moneypenny can be the stronger fit if trained external receptionists can take sensitive calls under clear instructions. The business should review agent training, scripts, escalation timing, and how human calls are billed.

LobbyStack can fit if the AI should collect only approved information and transfer every sensitive call to an on-call employee. Self-hosting may help the operator set stricter data controls, but it does not supply the missing person.

The best product is the one that respects the emotional and operational demands of the call.

## Ask who handles the exception

Map the calls that the AI should not finish. Include a distressed caller, a request outside policy, an emergency transfer that nobody answers, and a caller who refuses to share details. Ask Moneypenny whether its AI passes those calls to its receptionists, your staff, or a message queue. Confirm the hours and fees for each route.

Configure the same boundaries in LobbyStack. Your employee needs a clear on-call schedule and enough context to take over. Test the transcript, summary, caller consent, and fallback if the employee does not answer.

Moneypenny's higher per-call price may include human capacity that your company lacks. LobbyStack gives your team control over the automation and handoff, but your team supplies the human response. Price that staffing before choosing.
