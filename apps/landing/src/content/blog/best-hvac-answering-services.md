## The best live HVAC answering services

All seven still put people on HVAC calls in 2026. Six of them also appear in our [answering service pricing guide](/blog/how-much-does-an-answering-service-cost/).

### A Message Center: best HVAC-focused live service

A Message Center's [HVAC page](https://amessagecenter.com/hvac-answering-service) covers no-heat and no-cool triage and maintenance-plan visits. After hours, agents dispatch the on-call tech by phone, text or dispatch app, and different techs can cover different nights. The page names ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, FieldEdge and ServiceFusion. Starter costs $47 a month plus $2.00 a call, and Standard costs $145 for 50 calls ([pricing page](https://amessagecenter.com/pricing)). You pay the same for a 10-minute no-heat call as for a 1-minute one. Watch the extras: the FAQ says holidays, outcalls and patching may cost more but lists no amounts, and the site doesn't state a setup fee, contract term or Spanish coverage.

### AnswerForce: best for software integrations

AnswerForce uses human agents only, in English and Spanish. Its [HVAC page](https://www.answerforce.com/hvac-answering-service) says agents capture emergency details, dispatch the on-call technician and book estimates. It names 15 tools, from ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro and Jobber to Salesforce. On the [plans page](https://www.answerforce.com/plans-direct), the $349 Intro plan carries a $75 setup fee, and the larger plans don't. AnswerForce rounds calls up to the minute, but the first 30 interactions under 30 seconds in each cycle cost nothing.

### PATLive: best published rate card

PATLive's [pricing page](https://www.patlive.com/pricing/) lists 11 tiers up to 5,000 minutes, starting at $49 a month plus $2.99 a minute, with overage falling at each step. It bills the first minute in full, then in 6-second steps, and doesn't bill after-call notes. Nights and holidays cost the listed price. Spanish costs nothing extra, and its [HVAC page](https://www.patlive.com/industries/hvac) puts Spanish hours at 8am to 10pm ET. That page also covers dispatch, booking in your field service software and transfers by each person's real-time status. The only one-time fee we found is $50 to turn on business texting.

### MAP Communications: best low overage with an on-call roster

MAP Communications' overage falls to $1.28 a minute on its $339 and $649 plans ([pricing page](https://www.mapcommunications.com/pricing/)). Its FAQ describes a Roster Service: you supply the on-call schedule and agents follow it. The [HVAC page](https://www.mapcommunications.com/industries/contractors/hvac-answering-service/) covers triage, scheduling and dispatch, and every plan includes English and Spanish. MAP's [terms](https://www.mapcommunications.com/terms/) (March 2026) round calls up to whole minutes and count after-call work toward them. They also add a $0.024 telecom fee per call and a holiday fee on seven holidays, with no amount stated.

### Specialty Answering Service: best per-second billing

Specialty Answering Service (SAS) bills by the second, from $44 a month plus $1.54 a minute, with $0 setup and no contract ([pricing page](https://www.specialtyansweringservice.net/pricing/)). The trial runs 14 days or 200 minutes with no card. Its [HVAC page](https://www.specialtyansweringservice.net/industries/construction/hvac-answering-service/) says SAS handles on-call staff changes and complex schedules, reaches techs by phone, text, email or pager, and can take payment before dispatch. Patching a caller through to you costs $0.10 a minute. SAS offers Spanish-speaking agents, but its pages quote two prices, so get the figure in writing.

### Ruby: best for Spanish at any hour

Ruby's plans differ only in minutes, from $250 for 50 to $1,725 for 500 ([pricing page](https://www.ruby.com/plans-and-pricing/)). Every plan includes Spanish 24/7, and Ruby's app warns you at 85% and 100% of your minutes. Ruby charges no setup or after-hours fees and doesn't bill robocalls its filter catches. The [HVAC page](https://www.ruby.com/industries/hvac/) says agents escalate emergency calls to your team. Included minutes work out to $3.45 to $5.00 each, the highest per-minute price here, and Ruby publishes no overage rate. Calls round up to 60 seconds (terms, March 2026).

### Nexa: price on request for calls, texts and chats

Nexa sells 100, 300 and 500-minute plans and quotes prices on request ([plans page](https://www.nexa.com/plans/)). It charges a setup fee but doesn't give the amount. Nexa lists English and Spanish, and its [HVAC page](https://www.nexa.com/industries/hvac/) covers calls, texts and chats, with CRM integration on Nexa 500. The page describes no emergency handling and names no field service software, so ask for the dispatch steps in writing with your quote.

## Is there an AI answering service for HVAC technicians?

Yes. Jobber and Housecall Pro sell AI receptionists inside their own software. Sameday AI and Avoca connect to ServiceTitan as certified apps. Smith.ai puts live receptionists behind its AI. Goodcall, Rosie and LobbyStack book into a calendar and transfer urgent calls to people you name, Rosie from its $149 plan.

Vendors sell these as an AI receptionist, an HVAC virtual receptionist or an AI CSR. [Avoca](https://www.avoca.ai/) quotes prices on request and aims at larger shops. It routes emergencies 24/7 and can hand calls to Avoca's human CSR team.

### Smith.ai: best AI with live receptionists behind it

Smith.ai's [AI Receptionist](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist) charges $3.00 a call after the 25 free ones, or $150 for 75 calls on Pro. It speaks English and Spanish, and the pricing page lists no fee when the AI escalates to a live agent. The human [Virtual Front Desk](https://smith.ai/pricing/receptionists) costs $300 for 30 calls and charges $1.50 a call extra for booking. Neither product has a setup fee. The help center has setup guides for ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro and Jobber, and Smith.ai's HVAC article (April 2026) says safety-critical calls should go to a live receptionist at any hour.

### Jobber Receptionist: best for Jobber shops

Jobber Receptionist is a $29 monthly add-on with 30 conversations, then $0.79 each. Jobber Plus includes unlimited use at $399 a month billed annually ([help article](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/receptionistpowered-by-jobber-ai/), July 2026). You need a Jobber plan and a Jobber phone number in the US, Canada or the UK. It answers calls and texts 24/7, books into the Jobber calendar, and sends calls with your trigger words to an escalation contact. Spam doesn't count toward the limit.

### Housecall Pro CSR AI: best for Housecall Pro shops

Housecall Pro's help center calls [CSR AI](https://www.housecallpro.com/features/ai-team/csr-ai/) an optional paid add-on, and we found no price. It answers calls and chats 24/7, books in Housecall Pro, and escalates to a number you choose or to a Housecall Pro Assist Rep. Base plans start at $59 a month billed annually.

### Sameday AI: best for ServiceTitan shops with a budget

Sameday AI starts at $449 a month for 500 minutes and $789 for 1,000, and doesn't publish an overage rate ([pricing page](https://sameday.ai/pricing)). It's a certified ServiceTitan app that also connects to Housecall Pro, FieldRoutes and Service Fusion, and it books into the software during the call. Its nights-and-weekends "Emergency line" queue rings every member, and it offers round-robin queues. Multilingual voices come only on Enterprise.

### Goodcall: best for many repeat callers

Goodcall bills per unique caller and doesn't meter minutes: $79 a month covers 100 unique callers ([pricing page](https://www.goodcall.com/pricing)). A customer who calls three times in a month about one no-cool job counts once, and spam and wrong numbers don't count. The agent transfers callers it can't help to an escalation contact and can try up to three contacts if nobody answers. Goodcall's [FAQ](https://www.goodcall.com/faq) lists native connections to Google Calendar, Google Sheets and HighLevel; other systems, ServiceTitan included, need an API tool or MCP server you set up. The same FAQ limits Zapier to older GC3 agents, so ask whether the Zapier links to ServiceTitan, Jobber and Housecall Pro on its May 2026 [HVAC page](https://www.goodcall.com/answering-services/hvac-company) apply to a new account. Goodcall doesn't publish its languages.

### Rosie: best low-cost message taking

Rosie's $49 plan books only by texted link and has no transfers. The $149 plan adds calendar booking and live transfers, and the $299 plan adds waterfall transfers that try several numbers ([pricing page](https://heyrosie.com/pricing)). Going over moves you up a plan. Rosie speaks English and Spanish, and its [HVAC page](https://heyrosie.com/industries/hvac) says she "can't dispatch technicians directly". See [how Rosie's plans compare](/blog/rosie-ai-alternative/).

LobbyStack's Starter plan costs $30 a month for 150 minutes, then $0.20 a minute. Pro costs $100 for 500 minutes, then $0.18. Billed annually, they cost $24 and $80 a month, and no plan has a setup fee. LobbyStack counts usage in seconds, and calls under 10 seconds or calls it ends as spam don't count. It doesn't sync with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro or Jobber, and Google Calendar is its only calendar. Transfers go to one number without a briefing, and you get no on-call rotation. No human agents back it up, and the AI doesn't reply to texts. Free gives you 30 browser voice minutes with no card and no telephone number, for testing. ## Is an HVAC answering service worth it?

It pays off when the jobs you'd save cover the bill. Multiply the calls you miss in a month by the share that would have booked and by your average margin per job, then compare that with the month's price above. Run it for July and for April, because HVAC call volume follows the weather.

Take an example with made-up inputs: 20 missed calls, 1 in 4 of them booking and $300 of margin per job come to $1,500. A 100-call month costs $60 to $915 on the services with complete prices above. Try your own numbers in the [missed-call revenue calculator](/missed-call-revenue-calculator/).

Most of a plan sized for July goes unused in April. Pay-as-you-go plans at A Message Center, SAS, MAP and PATLive keep the slow-month bill low. SAS and PATLive let you change plans for the next month, and LobbyStack lets you upgrade or downgrade from billing settings.

## How do you choose an HVAC answering service?

### 1. Count your calls by month

Pull last July's and last April's call counts and average call length from your phone bill, then price both months. Per-call and per-caller plans need the count, and per-minute plans need the minutes.

### 2. Place three test calls before you sign

Call each finalist three times: a 2 a.m. no-heat call with a baby in the house, a tune-up booking, and a Spanish-speaking caller. Check who answered, what the summary said, and whether the on-call tech got the call. Trials: PATLive 14 days, SAS 14 days or 200 minutes, MAP and Housecall Pro free trials, Rosie 7 days, Smith.ai's AI Free plan, and LobbyStack Free, which runs in the browser for 30 minutes.

### 3. Read the terms for billing and fees

A 20-second wrong number bills as a full minute at MAP, PATLive, Ruby and Rosie, and at AnswerForce after its first 30 short calls each cycle. SAS bills it by the second, and LobbyStack counts 20 seconds. Then check holiday, telecom and setup fees, and notice periods: MAP wants written notice a month before your next invoice.

### 4. Keep your number with call forwarding

Forward your existing number from your carrier for overflow, after-hours or every call, so the number on your trucks stays the same. Jobber Receptionist needs a Jobber phone number. With LobbyStack, your carrier's forwarding rule decides which calls it answers, and you can ask the team to port your number instead.

## HVAC answering service FAQ

### Is there a free HVAC answering service?

No live service in this list is free; trials come closest. Smith.ai's AI Free plan covers 25 real calls a month. LobbyStack Free gives you 30 browser voice minutes a month with no telephone number, so it tests the receptionist but can't answer your business line.

### Can I use an HVAC answering service only after hours?

Yes. Set your carrier to forward calls when you close. PATLive, Ruby and Smith.ai say they charge the same rate after hours. LobbyStack answers whatever your carrier forwards, so your forwarding rule sets its hours.

### Can an HVAC answering service answer in Spanish?

Yes. AnswerForce, PATLive, MAP, Ruby, Nexa, Rosie and Smith.ai's AI plans list English and Spanish, and Smith.ai's live plans charge $1.00 a call for a dedicated Spanish line. SAS offers Spanish but quotes two prices for it. LobbyStack opens each call in English or French, then answers in the caller's language across 70+ languages, Spanish included.

### What happens if the on-call tech doesn't pick up?

Goodcall can try up to three escalation contacts, Rosie's $299 plan tries several numbers, Sameday AI's emergency line rings every member, and MAP's agents work from the roster you supply. LobbyStack's receptionist leaves the call once the tech's phone starts ringing. If the tech doesn't pick up, the caller reaches that phone's voicemail, if it has one. If the transfer doesn't go through, LobbyStack tells the caller, offers to take a message and can send your team a "Live call transfer failed" alert. We didn't find an answer on the other vendors' pages, so ask during the trial.
