## How do AI receptionists charge?

AI receptionists bill by the minute, the call, the unique caller or the interaction. Per-minute plans favor short calls, per-call plans favor long ones, and per-caller plans favor businesses whose customers call back often. Check what each vendor counts as billable before you compare two prices.

### Per minute

LobbyStack, RingCentral AIR, Dialzara, Zoom, Rosie, CloudTalk and AI-Receptionist.com sell bundles of minutes. ElevenLabs Reception sells credits at 1 per phone minute. My AI Front Desk sells 200 minutes, then draws extra minutes from a credit pool at 25 credits a minute.

### Per call

Upfirst, Aira, Smith.ai, Moneypenny AI and Quo Sona charge per call, so a 10-minute call costs the same as a 1-minute one. Aira bills completed calls, and Quo Sona spends 100 credits on each call.

### Per caller or per interaction

Goodcall counts each phone number once a month, however often it calls, and doesn't meter minutes. Nextiva XBert counts a call over 30 seconds, or a text or chat thread with 3 or more messages, as one interaction. Allo charges a flat price per receptionist and doesn't say how much usage that includes.

### What counts as a billable call

- Upfirst doesn't bill spam, calls under 15 seconds or callers who never speak. Quo Sona skips calls under 15 seconds, and Nextiva XBert skips calls under 30 seconds.
- Smith.ai filters known spammers and lets you remove up to 10% of a cycle's calls as spam. Goodcall doesn't count spam or wrong numbers.
- RingCentral AIR rounds each call up to the next 30 seconds.
- LobbyStack counts seconds without rounding up, so a 90-second call uses 1.5 minutes. Calls under 10 seconds and calls the receptionist ends as spam don't count.

The other vendors state no rule on the pages we read, so ask before you assume spam is free.

## How much can an AI receptionist save you?

An AI receptionist saves money when the calls it answers turn into work you would otherwise lose. A business that misses 8 calls a week, with a $300 average job, has about $1,238 a month at risk under the assumptions below. Recovering 25% to 50% of that is worth $310 to $619 a month before the plan cost.

### Count only the calls that could have booked

Remove spam, wrong numbers, vendors and existing customers with questions that can wait. If you missed 40 calls last month, don't count 40 lost customers. Count the callers who might have booked, ordered or asked for a quote if someone had picked up. Each of those calls costs you time too: the voicemail, the callback and the second callback.

### Run the missed call formula

LobbyStack's [missed call revenue calculator](/missed-call-revenue-calculator/) uses this formula:

```text
monthly revenue at risk =
missed calls per week x 4.3 x opportunity rate x booking rate x average job or order value
```

Take 8 missed calls a week, 40% of them real opportunities, 30% of those booking, and a $300 average job. That gives 8 x 4.3 x 0.40 x 0.30 x $300, or about **$1,238 a month at risk**. Some of those callers would have called back or turned out to be a poor fit. If better answering recovers 25% to 50%, you keep about **$310 to $619 a month** before the plan cost.

### A worked example

A local service business misses 35 calls in a month. After checking caller ID and voicemails, the owner counts 15 likely new customers. In a typical month, five of them would book, and the average job is $275.

```text
5 likely bookings x $275 average job value = $1,375/month
```

If better answering saves only 30% of that, you recover about $413 a month.

### Subtract the plan cost

```text
net monthly impact =
recovered revenue + avoided answering cost - AI receptionist plan cost
```

Say you recover $450 a month in revenue, stop paying $150 a month for outside answering help, and pay $30 for LobbyStack Starter. The net impact is about $570 a month.

Before you decide, test three recovery rates: 10%, 25% and 50%. On the $1,238 example, those give $124, $310 and $619 a month. If the plan only pays off at 80% recovery, your estimate is too optimistic. If it pays off at 10%, you risk little.

## When does an AI receptionist pay for itself?

An AI receptionist pays for itself when the work it recovers is worth more than the plan. If one booked job is worth $300, one recovered booking covers LobbyStack Starter ($30) or Pro ($100) for the month. Few calls, low ticket values or calls that need a licensed professional shrink the savings.

### Signs it will pay off

- Your team misses calls during business hours.
- Customers call after hours or on weekends.
- One booked job is worth more than the monthly plan.
- Callers ask the same questions before they book.
- You spend evenings on voicemails and callbacks.

### Signs it won't save much

- You get a few calls a month and answer nearly all of them.
- Your average sale is around $40 and most missed calls can wait.
- Most calls need a licensed professional, a custom quote or a sensitive conversation.
- Most of your missed calls are spam, vendors or existing customers.
- Your numbers only work at 80% recovery.

## FAQ

### Is an AI receptionist cheaper than an answering service?

For most small businesses, yes. At 40 three-minute calls a month, the AI plans we priced on October 9, 2026 cost $25.25 to $99, and live answering services cost $127 to $720. A live service costs more because a person takes every call. That's worth paying for when callers need human judgment.

### Do spam calls count toward my minutes?

It depends on the vendor. Upfirst doesn't bill spam or calls under 15 seconds, Goodcall doesn't count spam or wrong numbers, and Smith.ai lets you remove up to 10% of calls as spam. On LobbyStack, calls under 10 seconds and calls the receptionist ends as spam don't count, and other calls do.

### Do unused minutes roll over?

Not on LobbyStack. Included minutes reset each month, on monthly and annual billing alike. Quo Sona credits refresh monthly without rolling over, and RingCentral AIR minutes don't roll over either. Smith.ai mentions a 6-month plan with rollover calls but publishes no price for it, so ask its sales team.

### Can an AI receptionist answer calls in Spanish?

Many can. Smith.ai, Rosie and Dialzara list English and Spanish. Upfirst lists 35+ languages, My AI Front Desk 20+, Zoom 23, and CloudTalk and ElevenLabs Reception 70+. LobbyStack opens each call in English or French, then answers in the caller's language: 70+ languages on OpenAI GPT-Live, including Spanish and Serbian.

### How many missed calls before it pays for itself?

It depends on what a customer is worth. If one booked job brings in $300, one recovered booking covers LobbyStack Starter ($30) or Pro ($100). If each sale is worth $40, you need one recovered sale a month for Starter and three for Pro, before counting the time you save on callbacks.

### Will callers care that it is AI?

Some will, if the call feels evasive or they can't reach a person. Callers who want a booking or a quick answer need a clear next step. Keep the greeting short, say what the receptionist can do, and transfer the call or take a message when the caller needs a person.
