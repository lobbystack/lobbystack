## What is an AI phone system?

An AI phone system is a voice agent that answers your business number in natural language. Callers say what they need in their own words instead of pressing 1 or 2. The agent answers from the information you give it (hours, prices, service area), takes a message or books an appointment, and transfers the calls you choose to a person.

It often gets confused with two other tools:

- **An interactive voice response (IVR) menu**: a keypad menu that routes the call without handling it. Our guide to the [automated answering service](/blog/automated-answering-service/) compares menus, voicemail and AI.
- **A virtual phone system**: cloud telephony that rings your team's desk phones and apps, with queues and menus. Ringover, Aircall and 3CX sell these and also offer an AI agent.

This guide compares AI agents that work on their own, with your current line or a number they provide.

## What does an AI phone system do for a small business?

It takes the calls your team can't: during a job, an appointment or after closing time. It answers common questions, books into your calendar, logs quote requests and transfers urgent calls. Depending on the plan, you get a summary or a transcript of every call.

Its limits, worth knowing before you hand it your line:

- It follows your instructions. A question your information doesn't cover ends in a message or a transfer.
- It can mishear a name, an address or a caller in a noisy place. Read the transcripts during the first week.
- An angry customer or a sensitive matter needs a person. Decide in advance who takes the transfer.

## Does an AI phone system need a French number?

For a business in France, the simplest setup keeps your current number: you forward it to the AI phone system and your customers notice nothing. Your carrier bills forwarding as a call to the destination number. Forwarding to a French number falls within many plans. Forwarding to a foreign number is billed at international rates unless your plan covers that country.

Three setups:

- **A French number provided.** Konten includes a French number. Callsens, Tala and AirAgent include a number without naming the country on their pricing pages, so ask.
- **Your own number, forwarded.** Elio works through forwarding from your carrier and publishes forwarding codes for the main French carriers.
- **A foreign number.** LobbyStack offers numbers in the US, Canada, the UK, Australia and New Zealand, and nowhere else. Forwarding from your French line goes abroad.

Our guide on [how to forward business calls](/blog/how-to-forward-business-calls/) explains the types: all calls, when busy, or when unanswered.

## How do you choose an AI phone system?

1. **List your call reasons.** Write down the ten most common questions and the calls that must go to a person.
2. **Work out your minutes.** Multiply monthly calls by their average length, then apply each plan's bundle and extra-minute rate.
3. **Check the number and the forwarding.** Ask which country the provided number is in, and ask your carrier what forwarding costs.
4. **Test the transfer.** Have a call transferred to your mobile and see what happens if you don't answer.
5. **Test with real calls.** Use the trials: 100 minutes at Konten, 7 days at Tala and Callsens, 30 browser minutes at LobbyStack.
6. **Read the transcripts in the first week.** Fix your instructions wherever the agent hesitates.

### How is an AI phone system different from a phone answering service?

A phone answering service puts a person on the line and charges per call; an AI phone system answers at any hour and charges per minute. For 40 three-minute calls, expect €50 to €118.60 before VAT with a French answering service, versus €23.99 to €79 with an AI phone system. A person handles sensitive calls better.

### Can an AI phone system transfer calls to a person?

Yes, all five providers in this guide describe transfers to your team. You decide when: urgent calls, caller requests or certain hours. At LobbyStack, transfers go to one number. If a transfer fails, the receptionist tells the caller and offers to take a message, and your team can get an alert.

### Do I need to switch phone carriers?

No. You keep your line and forward it to the AI phone system's number: all calls, or only the ones you don't answer. Your carrier bills forwarding as a call to the destination number. For a foreign number, such as LobbyStack's, ask your carrier for the exact rate before you switch it on.
