## How do you choose an answering service for a plumbing company?

### 1. Count your calls in a freeze month and a slow month

Pull call counts and average call length for your busiest and quietest months from your phone bill, then price both. Per-call and per-caller plans need the count, and per-minute plans need the minutes. Pay-as-you-go plans at A Message Center, SAS, MAP and PATLive keep the slow-month bill low.

### 2. Place three test calls before you sign

Call each finalist three times: a 2 a.m. burst pipe with water still running, a sewer backup in a basement, and a water heater quote. Make one of them in Spanish if your customers speak it. Check who answered, what the summary said, and whether the on-call plumber got the call. Trials: PATLive 14 days, SAS 14 days or 200 minutes, Rosie 7 days, free trials at MAP and Housecall Pro, Smith.ai's AI Free plan, and LobbyStack Free, with 30 minutes and a US or Canadian number free for 14 days.

### 3. Read the terms for billing and fees

A 20-second wrong number bills as a full minute at MAP, PATLive, Ruby and Rosie, and at AnswerForce after its first 30 short calls each cycle. SAS bills it by the second, and LobbyStack counts 20 seconds. Then check holiday, telecom and setup fees and the notice period. MAP wants written notice a month before your next invoice.

### 4. Keep your number with call forwarding

Forward your existing number from your carrier for overflow, after-hours or every call, so the number on your vans stays the same. Jobber Receptionist needs calls forwarded to a Jobber phone number. With LobbyStack, your carrier's forwarding rule decides which calls it answers. Our guide on [how to forward business calls](/blog/how-to-forward-business-calls/) covers the common carriers.
