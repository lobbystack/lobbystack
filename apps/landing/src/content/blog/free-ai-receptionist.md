## What does "free" mean for an AI receptionist?

Vendors use "free" for four different offers. For each one, ask two questions: can it answer calls to your real business number, and for how long?

| Kind of free | What you get | What ends it | Examples (October 2026) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free plan | A small allowance every month, with no end date | Using up the month's calls or minutes | Smith.ai, HireJosie, LobbyStack |
| Free trial | A paid plan for a set number of days | The trial clock, or the trial's minutes | Upfirst, ElevenLabs Reception, ModPhone |
| Starter credit | A few dollars of usage on a voice AI platform | The credit balance | Vapi ($5), Retell AI ($10) |
| Open source | Code you run on your own server | Nothing, but your providers bill you | LobbyStack (MIT license) |

A fifth kind comes bundled with software you already pay for. Quo's Sona agent includes 1,000 credits a month, about 10 calls, but only on a [paid Quo plan](https://support.quo.com/core-concepts/ai-automations/sona/sona-pricing), which starts at $19 per user a month ($15 billed annually).

### What to test during a free trial

You learn the most from a trial that runs on real calls. Before the clock ends:

1. **Forward a slice of real calls.** Send overflow or after-hours calls from your business number to the trial number. Our guide on [how to forward business calls](/blog/how-to-forward-business-calls/) covers the carrier settings.
2. **Call it with your three hardest questions.** Ask for a price range, report an emergency and ask to move an appointment.
3. **Check where the work lands.** Look for the booking in your calendar and the message in your inbox, and listen to what the caller hears when a transfer doesn't connect.
4. **Read the billing terms.** Note the unit (per call, per minute or per unique caller) and what happens past the allowance. Rosie, for example, moves you up to its next plan instead of charging per extra minute.

## Can you build a free AI receptionist yourself?

You can, until the credit runs out. Voice AI platforms give new accounts starter credit: Vapi gives $5 and Retell AI gives $10 (checked October 10, 2026). Both bill per minute after that.

- **[Vapi](https://vapi.ai/pricing)** charges $0.05 a minute for hosting, plus the language model, speech and voice providers at cost. If you connect a Twilio number, Twilio bills its minutes too. $5 covers 100 minutes of the hosting fee alone, before those other charges. The pay-as-you-go plan includes one Vapi phone number.
- **[Retell AI](https://www.retellai.com/pricing)** lists voice agents at $0.07 to $0.31 a minute and phone numbers at $2 a month. $10 covers about 32 to 143 minutes, depending on the models you pick.

These platforms give you a voice agent. You build the rest: prompts, calendar booking, call logs, alerts and transfer rules, usually wired together with webhooks or an automation tool. That suits you if you like building. If you need the phone answered this week, it's slow.

## Is an open-source AI receptionist free?

You get the code for free and pay the running costs yourself. LobbyStack's code is MIT-licensed, so you can self-host it with Docker Compose or a Railway template and pay LobbyStack nothing. A self-hosted install has no LobbyStack plan limits on minutes.

You still pay for:

- a server for the app, database and background worker
- [Twilio](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/voice/pricing/us) phone numbers and call minutes
- [OpenAI](https://openai.com/api/pricing/) usage for GPT-Live, the voice model that talks to callers
- the hours it takes you to deploy, update and watch it

Twilio and OpenAI process the call audio while a call is live, on a self-hosted install too. Self-hosting suits a business with a technical person on staff, or an agency that wants to run receptionists for clients. ## When does a free AI receptionist stop being enough?

A free option stops being enough when it can't take the calls you'd otherwise miss. One of four limits decides it:

1. **It can't answer your real number, or not for long.** Demo agents, such as Goodcall's, prove the voice and the rules but don't cover your line. LobbyStack Free covers it for 14 days.
2. **The cap is smaller than your missed calls.** Fifteen minutes covers about five 3-minute calls. Twenty-five calls is less than one a day.
3. **The trial ends.** A 7-day trial may not include your busiest day.
4. **You need booking, transfers or texts.** Check which plan includes them. Rosie's $49 plan texts callers a booking link instead of booking into your calendar and doesn't transfer calls. Both start on its $149 plan.

## Questions about free AI receptionists

### Is there a free AI answering service?

Yes, though few answer real calls for free. As of October 2026, Smith.ai's free plan covers 25 real calls a month, and HireJosie's covers 15 minutes with a dedicated number. Most other AI answering services offer 7- to 30-day trials. Some free plans are browser tests with no phone number. LobbyStack's adds a US or Canadian number for 14 days.

### Can a free AI receptionist answer my business number?

Some can. Smith.ai's and HireJosie's free plans take real calls, and the trials from Upfirst, ElevenLabs Reception and ModPhone take real calls while they last. You keep your existing number and set your carrier to forward calls to the AI's number. LobbyStack Free answers real calls on a US or Canadian number for 14 days. Goodcall's demo agent can't answer a phone line.

### Do free AI receptionists need a credit card?

Many don't. HireJosie's free plan and the trials from ModPhone, Upfirst, ElevenLabs Reception, CloudTalk, PhoneFlow, AI-Receptionist.com and Rosie say you don't need a card, and LobbyStack Free doesn't ask for one either. Smith.ai, RingCentral, Nextiva, Dialzara, My AI Front Desk and Zoom didn't say on the pages we checked.

### What happens when the free minutes run out?

It depends on the vendor. HireJosie's free plan switches calls to voicemail mode, ElevenLabs Reception's trial stops answering, and LobbyStack Free stops test calls until the next month. Smith.ai charges $3.00 for each call past 25. Before you rely on a free tier, find out whether it stops, charges you or moves you to a bigger plan.

### Is a free AI receptionist enough for a small business?

Usually only for testing. A business that misses five calls a week misses about 22 calls a month. That's well past HireJosie's 15 minutes and close to Smith.ai's 25 calls. Use free plans to check the voice and your rules, then pay for a plan sized to your missed calls.
