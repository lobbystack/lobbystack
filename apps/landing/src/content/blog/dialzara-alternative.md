## LobbyStack includes more receptionist for the money

One extra dollar changes the entry allowance from Dialzara's 60 minutes to LobbyStack's 150. At about $100, LobbyStack includes 500 minutes against Dialzara's 220. LobbyStack also includes alert SMS segments, while Dialzara lists SMS as a $19 monthly add-on plus usage.

## Dialzara gives buyers several commercial products

Dialzara sells more than one receptionist plan. Its public site separates inbound AI reception from outbound calling, SMS, chat, agency, and white-label products. That structure gives agencies a vendor-managed route to package phone automation for clients.

The inbound plans advertise recordings, summaries, notifications, knowledge uploads, transfers, calendar synchronization, multilingual calls, Make, Zapier, and API access on higher tiers.

LobbyStack offers an affiliate program, a managed cloud, and an open-source stack. An agency can refer the hosted product or operate a deployment, but self-hosting requires technical staff.

## API access and source access solve different problems

An API lets a team connect a hosted service to other software. Source access lets the team inspect and change the service itself.

Dialzara advertises API access on higher tiers and connects to Make and Zapier. That can cover CRM updates, alerts, and downstream automation without operating the phone platform.

LobbyStack exposes APIs and publishes the application code. A self-hosted operator can choose providers, change integrations, manage retention, and deploy updates on its schedule. The team also becomes responsible for uptime, backups, security, and call testing.

Buyers who only need a webhook should not take on self-hosting. Buyers with infrastructure or customization requirements should not assume API access gives the same control as source access.

## An automation agency example

An agency serves plumbers and electricians. Each client needs a phone number, business knowledge, after-hours routing, calendar booking, and text alerts.

Dialzara can fit if the agency wants a published white-label or agency product and prefers vendor-managed infrastructure. The agency should confirm client administration, billing separation, export, and which plan contains each feature.

LobbyStack can fit if the agency wants to deploy a common codebase with client-specific provider accounts and rules. The agency should calculate its operations labor alongside voice and model costs.

The margin depends on support and maintenance, not only the subscription price.

## Price the complete client workflow

Build one quote for the work your client expects. Include the inbound plan, SMS add-on, carrier fees, calendar and transfer tier, white-label tools, and any outbound campaign. Ask Dialzara which limits apply per client, agent, number, or agency account.

Build the LobbyStack quote from voice minutes, SMS segments, phone-provider charges, and the engineering time for custom changes. A self-hosted deployment also needs monitoring, backups, upgrades, and incident response.

Test a booking that needs rescheduling and a transfer outside business hours. Then trigger an SMS and webhook. Your agency should compare the finished client record and the labor required to support it, not one line from either pricing page.
