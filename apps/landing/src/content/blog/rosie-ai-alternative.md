Calendar booking, transfers and texts during the call start on Scale. Go over 250 minutes and Rosie moves you to Scale until you downgrade.

## What is Rosie AI answering service?

Rosie (heyrosie.com) is a hosted AI receptionist for small businesses. It answers calls 24/7, trains itself on your Google Business Profile and website, and speaks English and Spanish on every call. Every plan includes native iOS and Android apps, free website chat, Zapier, call summaries, transcripts and recordings, 10+ voices and simultaneous calls.

For now, Rosie reaches your other tools through Zapier. Its FAQ says direct CRM integrations are in progress.

## How much does Rosie AI answering service cost?

Rosie AI pricing has three self-serve plans, billed by the month or the year. Paying for a year saves the price of two months. [Rosie's pricing page](https://heyrosie.com/pricing) listed these plans on October 9, 2026:

| Plan | Monthly | Annual, per month | Minutes | Calendar booking | Transfers | Texts during the call | Message scenarios | Also included |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Professional | $49 | $41 ($490 a year) | 250 | Texts a booking link only | Not included | Not included | 2 | Spam detection, website chat widget |
| Scale | $149 | $124 ($1,490 a year) | 1,000 | Google Calendar, Calendly, Acuity, Appointlet | Warm and live | Yes | 5 | Everything in Professional |
| Growth | $299 | $249 ($2,990 a year) | 2,000 | Same as Scale | Warm, live and waterfall | Yes | Unlimited | Custom training from your documents, white-glove onboarding, priority support |

In a warm transfer, Rosie briefs your team before it connects the caller. A waterfall transfer tries several numbers until someone answers. Growth customers who pass 2,000 minutes month after month can ask Rosie for a custom-volume plan, priced on request.

### What happens when you go over your Rosie minutes?

Rosie charges no per-minute overage. Its FAQ says Rosie sends you several heads-up emails as you near your limit. If you go over, Rosie moves you to the next plan at that plan's standard price, and you stay there until you downgrade. Upgrades take effect at once. Downgrades wait for the start of the next billing cycle. On the $49 plan, minute 251 moves you to Scale, and your bill goes from $49 to $149. Neither the FAQ nor the terms say whether Rosie prorates the first upgraded month.

Rosie's [terms of service](https://heyrosie.com/legal/terms), last updated February 5, 2026, add two details the pricing page leaves out. Section 3(c) bills minutes "in 60-second increments" and rounds each call up to the next 60-second mark, so a 61-second call uses 2 minutes. Section 4(a) says a plan change that raises your fees applies to the current billing period "and at least the subsequent two consecutive billing periods." The FAQ doesn't mention that minimum. If it covers automatic upgrades, one busy month on Professional keeps you on Scale for at least three billing periods: $447 instead of $147. Ask Rosie how the clause applies before you count on downgrading the next month.

You may see a $0.25 per-minute Rosie overage quoted elsewhere. My AI Front Desk, another AI receptionist vendor, published that rate on September 24, 2025, along with Scale at $99 for 500 minutes and Growth at $199 for 1,000. Those figures don't match Rosie's pricing page or FAQ today.

### Does Rosie charge a setup fee or offer a free trial?

Rosie's pricing page lists no setup fee. Section 4(a) of its terms allows for setup or activation fees "as applicable" but names no amount.

Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. Rosie's FAQ says you can switch plans or cancel before the trial ends and pay nothing, and its terms turn the trial into a paid subscription if you don't cancel. Section 4(b) of the terms says you must keep a valid payment method on your account to use a free trial.

A Rosie subscription runs 30 days and renews unless you cancel. Rosie has no contracts or cancellation fees. You cancel from the admin and keep access to the end of the billing period.

Website chat is free on every plan. The Website Texting add-on costs $50 a month for 25 conversations, then $1 for each extra conversation.

### How Rosie's rounding changes the minute count

Rosie rounds each call up to the next minute, and LobbyStack counts seconds, so the same calls can use more Rosie minutes. Take 100 calls of 2 minutes 10 seconds each:

- Rosie counts 300 minutes (100 × 3). That's past the $49 plan's 250, so the month costs $149.
- LobbyStack counts 216.7 minutes (13,000 seconds). On Starter that costs $43.33: $30 plus 66.7 extra minutes at $0.20.

LobbyStack also leaves out calls under 10 seconds and calls the receptionist ends as spam. Rosie's pricing page doesn't say whether spam calls use minutes.

### A salon with 600 minutes a month

Assume a salon takes 240 calls a month, each lasting 2 minutes 30 seconds, and wants the receptionist to book appointments. LobbyStack counts 600 minutes. Rosie rounds each call up to 3 minutes and counts 720.

- **Rosie:** 720 minutes is past Professional's 250, and calendar booking needs Scale anyway. The salon pays $149 a month, or $124 a month on annual billing.
- **LobbyStack:** Pro costs $100 for 500 minutes, plus 100 × $0.18 = $18, for $118 a month. Starter would cost $120 ($30 + 450 × $0.20). On annual billing, Pro costs $80 plus the same $18, or $98 a month.

Before you pick, test how each product handles service length, stylist schedules, buffers and cancellations. LobbyStack books into Google Calendar only, for the whole business or for each staff member. Rosie Scale also connects Calendly, Acuity and Appointlet.

## Which languages do Rosie and LobbyStack answer in?

Rosie answers in English and Spanish on every call. LobbyStack opens each call in the business's default language, English or French. If the caller asks to switch or speaks another language, the receptionist answers in that language. LobbyStack [runs on OpenAI GPT-Live](/blog/ai-voice-agent-gpt-live/) and handles 70+ languages, including Spanish and Serbian.

CloudTalk and ElevenLabs Reception also state 70+ languages, so the count alone doesn't set LobbyStack apart. Rosie covers fewer languages, but Spanish is ready on every call. If most of your callers speak Spanish, test how they react to LobbyStack's English or French greeting before it switches. For your staff, LobbyStack's dashboard comes in English, French, Spanish and Serbian.

A language count doesn't prove quality on either product. Call each finalist with your customers' names, street addresses, interruptions and a switch of language halfway through the call.

## How do you set up and test a Rosie alternative?

1. Sign up at [app.lobbystack.com](https://app.lobbystack.com/en/signup). Free gives you 30 browser voice minutes a month without a card.
2. Setup walks you through your business details, a website import, your knowledge, the greeting and a plan.
3. Write your call rules in plain language and pick a booking mode: book the appointment, take a request for your team to confirm, or take a message. Connect Google Calendar and set your opening hours. Booking won't work without them.
4. Run test calls from the dashboard in your browser.
5. On Starter or Pro, pick a local number, or a toll-free one where available, in the US, Canada, the UK or Australia. To keep your existing number, set your carrier to forward calls to LobbyStack when you're busy or don't answer, when you're closed, or all the time. To port the number instead, contact the LobbyStack team. Then add a transfer number and choose when transfers happen.
6. Run the same five calls on Rosie's trial, on the plan you'd keep: a policy question, a booking that needs a buffer, an urgent caller who needs a transfer, a request for a confirmation text and a cancellation. Note which tasks work on that plan and which need Scale or Growth.

Rosie supports the same forwarding setup for overflow and after-hours calls. Read both sets of transcripts with the person on your team who handles the follow-up.
