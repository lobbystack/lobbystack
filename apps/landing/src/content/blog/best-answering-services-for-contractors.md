## Which answering service is best for contractors?

Start with the call that costs you the most when it reaches voicemail. For a remodeler, that's a kitchen estimate request. For a roofer, it's the flood of leak calls the morning after hail. Then check where the booking lands, because retyping every lead into Jobber or JobNimbus eats the time the service saved you.

- **PATLive, best for roofers and builders on AccuLynx, Buildertrend or Procore:** live agents book inspections and estimates, from $49 a month plus $2.99 a minute.
- **AnswerForce, best for remodelers on Improveit360, Jobber or Housecall Pro:** human agents in English and Spanish, $349 a month for 200 minutes.
- **AnswerHero, cheapest live minutes:** bilingual agents on every plan, $279 a month for 250 minutes and no setup fee.
- **Smith.ai, best for detailed estimate intake:** live or AI receptionists that ask about scope, budget, timeline and permits.
- **Jobber Receptionist, best for Jobber shops:** a $29 add-on with 30 conversations a month.
- **Sameday AI, best for roofers on JobNimbus or AccuLynx with a budget:** an AI phone system from $449 a month for 500 minutes.
- **OnCrew, lowest AI bill in our storm month:** $49 for 100 calls, then $0.99 a call.
- **LobbyStack (ours), best for Google Calendar shops on a small budget:** $30 a month for 150 minutes with no setup fee. It answers each caller in their own language, from 70+ languages.

## The best live answering services for contractors

Live agents handle the calls that don't fit a script, like a homeowner describing water coming through a ceiling light. They cost more per minute than AI, and a storm month multiplies the bill.

### PATLive: best for roofers and builders on AccuLynx, Buildertrend or Procore

PATLive's [construction page](https://www.patlive.com/industries/construction/) says its receptionists schedule service calls, inspections and estimates, screen out sales calls, and route by schedule, such as the dispatcher on weekdays and the on-call tech on weekends. It names ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, Service Fusion, FieldPulse, AccuLynx, Buildertrend and Procore, and its [HVAC page](https://www.patlive.com/industries/hvac) adds JobNimbus. The construction page mentions weather-related surges but gives no capacity figure.

The [pricing page](https://www.patlive.com/pricing/) lists 11 tiers, from $49 a month plus $2.99 a minute to 5,000 minutes, with $349 for 200 minutes. PATLive bills the first minute in full, then in 6-second steps, and charges the listed price on nights and holidays. Spanish costs nothing extra, the trial runs 14 days, and there's no contract.

### AnswerForce: best for remodelers on Improveit360, Jobber or Housecall Pro

AnswerForce's [contractor page](https://www.answerforce.com/contractors-answering-service) says agents screen and qualify leads and book estimates into your calendar, in English or Spanish. It names Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Workiz, Service Fusion, FieldPulse, Improveit360, Encircle, QuickBooks, HubSpot, Salesforce and Zapier. JobNimbus and AccuLynx don't appear on the contractor page or the [roofing page](https://www.answerforce.com/industries/roofing). After each call, a summary goes out by app, email or text.

Prices sit on an unlinked [plans page](https://www.answerforce.com/plans-direct): $349 for 200 minutes with a $75 setup fee, then $389 for 300 and $669 for 500 with no setup fee. Extra minutes cost $1.85 to $2.00. Calls round up to the minute, but the first 30 interactions under 30 seconds in each billing cycle cost nothing.

### AnswerHero: cheapest live minutes

AnswerHero's [pricing page](https://answerhero.com/pricing-plans/) has the lowest live rates in this list: $39 for 10 minutes, $79 for 50, $149 for 100, $279 for 250 and $499 for 500, with extra minutes falling from $1.99 to $1.25 across those plans. Every plan lists bilingual agents, and its [construction page](https://answerhero.com/industries/service-providers/construction-companies/) mentions Spanish-speaking callers. No plan has a setup fee, a contract, or weekend or holiday fees.

Three details raise the bill: calls round up to the quarter minute, live transfers cost $0.06 a minute, and cancelling takes 30 days' notice before the end of a billing cycle. The construction page covers 24/7 answering, lead capture, scheduling and urgent-call routing, but names no field service software. AnswerHero also sells an AI plan at $19 a month plus $0.79 a minute, and hybrid plans from $90.

### Smith.ai Virtual Front Desk: best for detailed estimate intake

Smith.ai's [contractor page](https://smith.ai/industries/home-office-contractors-answering-service) lists the intake its receptionists run: project type, residential or commercial, work needed, timeline, budget range, property location and access, and permit needs. Its AI schedules estimates and site visits, while live receptionists handle multi-step scheduling and emergencies that need dispatch.

The live [Virtual Front Desk](https://smith.ai/pricing/receptionists) costs $300 for 30 calls, then $11.50 a call, and booking adds $1.50 a call. A 12-minute remodel conversation costs the same as a 1-minute one, and spam costs nothing as long as your forwarded lines pass caller ID. There's no setup fee, and plans run month to month. Smith.ai's help center has setup guides for ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro and Jobber.

### Posh: first on Google, prices shown to us in Canadian dollars

Posh held the first organic spot for this search on October 10, 2026, and Google's snippet of its contractor page mentions lead capture, appointments and plans from $65 a month. We couldn't confirm that in US dollars. Posh served us its Canadian [pricing page](https://posh.com/pricing/), which lists CAD 65 a month plus CAD 2.30 a minute and CAD 215 for 100 minutes, effective January 1, 2026. The same page's FAQ quotes $64 plus $1.99 a minute.

Posh bills on a calendar-month cycle rather than every 28 days, has no long-term contract, and says every plan includes every feature. Ask for the US rate card in writing before you compare it with the others.

## What are some AI answering services available for contractors?

Jobber and Housecall Pro sell AI receptionists inside their own software. Sameday AI lists ServiceTitan, JobNimbus and AccuLynx among its connections. OnCrew is built for trades and bills per call. Smith.ai puts live receptionists behind its AI. LobbyStack books into Google Calendar and transfers urgent calls to one number. Pick the AI service that books where your jobs already live.

### Jobber Receptionist: best for Jobber shops

Jobber Receptionist is a $29 monthly add-on with 30 conversations, then $0.79 each, or you can pause it at the limit. Jobber Plus includes unlimited use and starts at $399 a month billed annually ([help article](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/receptionistpowered-by-jobber-ai/), updated July 9, 2026). You need a Jobber plan and a Jobber phone number in the US, Canada or the UK, and you can forward your existing number to it.

It books jobs into the Jobber calendar through your online booking settings and submits requests through your request form. Trigger words such as "emergency" send your escalation contact a text with a transcript snippet, or transfer the call right away. It answers texts too. Spam and calls with no real conversation don't count toward the limit. The help article doesn't list languages.

### Housecall Pro CSR AI: for Housecall Pro shops

[CSR AI](https://www.housecallpro.com/features/ai-team/csr-ai/) answers calls and chats 24/7, books jobs in Housecall Pro, and sends callers to a number you choose or to a Housecall Pro Assist Rep. Housecall Pro's own [guide to AI answering services](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/best-ai-answering-services-contractors/) says plans start at $59 a month with unlimited minutes, which matches its Basic plan price. Its help center calls CSR AI an optional paid add-on, though, and we found no add-on price. Ask what CSR AI adds to your bill before the free trial ends. The feature page says text answering isn't available yet.

### Sameday AI: best for roofers on JobNimbus or AccuLynx with a budget

Sameday's [homepage](https://sameday.ai/) lists JobNimbus, AccuLynx, ServiceTitan (as a certified app), Housecall Pro, FieldRoutes and Service Fusion, plus lead sources such as Thumbtack, Google Local Services and Angi. It links a roofing page and a roofing customer story. Its example setup includes an "Emergency line" that rings every team member on nights and weekends, and an after-hours leads queue.

Pricing starts at $449 a month for 500 minutes and $789 for 1,000 ([pricing page](https://sameday.ai/pricing)). Sameday publishes no overage rate, and multilingual voices come only on Enterprise. The pricing page names only ServiceTitan for scheduling, so ask Sameday to write a test job into JobNimbus or AccuLynx before you sign.

### OnCrew: lowest AI bill in our storm month

OnCrew answers for HVAC, plumbing, electrical and roofing contractors and bills per call: $49 for 100 calls, $149 for 400 and $349 for 1,000, then $0.99 a call ([pricing page](https://oncrew.ai/pricing)). Emergencies trigger a text and email to your on-call team with the transcript and recording. Live transfers announce the caller before connecting, and plans include 4, 6 or 16 transfer destinations. If nobody answers within about 25 seconds, it takes a message.

Spanish is a free setting, and its [AI answering page](https://oncrew.ai/ai-answering-service) says English and Spanish are the only languages. OnCrew syncs with Google Calendar. Housecall Pro connects only on Housecall Pro's MAX plan. Jobber and ServiceTitan receive one record per call through a webhook export, and OnCrew doesn't book onto their schedules. The 14-day trial needs no card, and setup is included.

### Smith.ai AI Receptionist: AI with live receptionists behind it

Smith.ai's [AI Receptionist](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist) has a Free plan with 25 calls a month, then $3.00 a call. Pro charges $2.00 a call for 75 calls ($150), $1.80 for 150 and $1.67 for 300, with extra calls at $2.17 to $2.50. Calls can transfer to a live receptionist in North America, and the pricing page lists no fee for that. Plans run month to month with 30 days' notice to cancel, and you can remove up to 10% of each cycle's calls from the bill as spam.

### LobbyStack (ours): best for Google Calendar shops on a small budget

LobbyStack's Starter plan costs $30 a month for 150 minutes, then $0.20 a minute. Pro costs $100 for 500 minutes, then $0.18. Billed annually, they cost $24 and $80 a month, and no plan has a setup fee. LobbyStack counts usage in seconds and doesn't bill spam or calls under 10 seconds. Plans don't limit how many calls it answers at once.

You get a number in the US, Canada, the UK, Australia or New Zealand and can forward your existing business number to it. It answers every call or only overflow or after-hours calls. It books estimate visits into Google Calendar and, with the caller's consent, texts a confirmation and a 24-hour reminder to US and Canadian numbers. Every call's summary, transcript and recording lands in the dashboard, and you get an email when it takes a message, with text alerts optional. Each call opens in the business's default language, English or French, then the receptionist answers in the caller's language: 70+ languages on OpenAI GPT-Live, Spanish included. The code is MIT open source, and you can self-host it.

It has no integration with Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, JobNimbus or any CRM, and Google Calendar is its only calendar. If a transfer can't go through, it tells the caller, offers to take a message, and your team can get a "Live call transfer failed" alert. No human agents back it up, and the AI doesn't reply to texts. Free gives you 30 voice minutes and a US or Canadian number free for 14 days, with no card. ## Is an answering service worth it for a contractor?

It pays when the jobs you'd save cover the bill. Count the estimate requests that reached voicemail last month, multiply by the share that would have signed and by your margin per job, then compare that with the month's price above.

Take an example with made-up inputs: 6 missed estimate requests, 1 in 3 signing and $1,500 of margin per job come to $3,000. A normal month costs $40 to $530 on the services with complete prices above. Try your own numbers in the [missed-call revenue calculator](/missed-call-revenue-calculator/).

Run the numbers for your slow season too. Low base fees keep a quiet month cheap: PATLive's pay-as-you-go plan starts at $49, AnswerHero's Economy plan at $39 and LobbyStack's Starter at $30.

## How do you choose an answering service for your trade?

### 1. Price your busiest and slowest months

Pull call counts and average call length for your busiest and slowest months from your phone bill. Per-call plans need the count, and per-minute plans need the minutes. Roofers should price a storm month.

### 2. Place three test calls before you sign

Call each finalist three times: a bathroom remodel estimate request, a 9 p.m. call about an active leak, and a Spanish-speaking caller. Check who answered, what the summary said, and whether the urgent call reached your phone. Trials: PATLive 14 days, OnCrew 14 days, Housecall Pro's free trial, Smith.ai's AI Free plan, and LobbyStack Free, with 30 minutes and a US or Canadian number free for 14 days.

### 3. Watch a booking land in your software

Ask each finalist to book a test estimate into Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, JobNimbus or AccuLynx while you watch. If it can't, price the office time it takes to retype each lead.

### 4. Keep your number with call forwarding

Forward your existing number from your carrier for overflow, after-hours or every call, so the number on your trucks and yard signs stays the same. Jobber Receptionist needs a Jobber phone number, and you forward your business line to it. With LobbyStack, your carrier's forwarding rule decides which calls it answers. Calls from your Google listing follow the same rule, and our guide to [Google Business Profile calls](/blog/google-business-profile-calls/) shows how to track them.
