Buyers searching for a **My AI Front Desk alternative** should start with the entry price. Frontdesk now sells one self-serve plan, Business-in-a-Box, at $99 a month for 200 voice minutes, or $79 a month billed annually. It offers a 7-day free trial and no permanent free plan. LobbyStack Starter costs $30 a month for 150 minutes, and LobbyStack Free includes 30 browser voice minutes a month, with appointment booking, call summaries, and call history.

Frontdesk, formerly My AI Front Desk, now sells a broad AI sales workspace. LobbyStack gives businesses a complete phone receptionist at a lower entry price, more included voice minutes at about $100 a month, and the choice of managed cloud or self-hosting.

## Test LobbyStack in your browser for free

Frontdesk's trial lasts seven days, and its FAQ rules out a permanent free plan. After the trial, the $99 plan is the cheapest way to keep a Frontdesk voice agent answering.

LobbyStack's Free plan includes 30 browser voice minutes a month and no telephone number. The plan doesn't expire, so you can test customer scenarios in your browser for as long as you need before choosing a paid plan. Starter and Pro include a dedicated number for telephone calls.

A seven-day trial suits a team that can finish its tests in one week. LobbyStack Free suits a team that wants to adjust greetings, rules, and knowledge over several weeks before it moves phone traffic.

## Frontdesk covers more channels, if you need them

Frontdesk has moved beyond a phone-only receptionist. Its product pages advertise inbound voice, automated outbound calls, two-way SMS, web chat, email replies, form capture, follow-up sequences, and a built-in CRM. It can also push contacts, transcripts, and summaries to HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, or GoHighLevel. A business can keep several early sales interactions in one vendor's system.

That scope helps a small team that has no CRM and wants one product to capture a web lead, text them, call them, and track the exchange. Frontdesk also sells white-label partner plans for agencies, which its pricing FAQ says start at $500 a month.

LobbyStack avoids charging receptionist customers for sales tools they may not need. It stores callers, appointments, messages, transcripts, summaries, and business rules, and books appointments into Google Calendar. Webhooks and the REST API send call and booking events to the tools you already use.

## Frontdesk now sells one $99 plan

Frontdesk's pricing page lists one self-serve plan, Business-in-a-Box, at $99 a month or $79 a month billed annually. It includes 200 voice minutes, and the FAQ says there are no separate Starter or Pro tiers. Partner and Enterprise pricing is custom.

Each extra voice minute costs 25 credits, or $0.25. The plan includes 1,000 credits a month, enough for about 40 extra minutes, and those credits expire at the end of the month. Auto-reload adds 1,000 credits for $10.

Business-in-a-Box also lists:

- 20 knowledge pages
- the last 200 interaction logs
- three notification recipients
- two editor seats

Those limits may be enough for a small operation. A team with a large knowledge base, a longer audit window, or more editors should ask about Partner or Enterprise pricing before moving phone traffic.

LobbyStack includes more phone usage at the same spending level:

- Free: 30 voice minutes a month in the browser, on a plan that doesn't expire
- Starter: 150 minutes for $30 a month, or $24 a month billed annually
- Pro: 500 minutes for $100 a month, compared with 200 on Frontdesk's $99 plan

At 200 minutes, LobbyStack Starter costs about $40 with its published $0.20 per-minute overage. Frontdesk charges $99 a month for the same included voice volume. At 240 minutes, about the most Frontdesk's $99 covers before auto-reload, Starter costs $48. Pro includes 500 minutes for $100, two and a half times Frontdesk's 200.

Frontdesk bundles more sales software into its $99 plan. That bundle creates value when a business needs those tools. A buyer who needs an AI receptionist gets more call capacity per dollar from LobbyStack.

## Focus can be an advantage

A broad product can reduce the number of vendors. It also asks a team to adopt more of Frontdesk's workflow. Frontdesk can sync records to HubSpot or Salesforce, so a team can keep its CRM, but the $99 price still covers chat, email, forms, and sequences that a phone-only buyer may never open.

LobbyStack handles the phone and leaves the CRM where it is. PostgreSQL holds the receptionist's durable business state, the Next.js app handles operator and API traffic, and a worker runs live calls on OpenAI GPT-Live. A self-hosted operator can control the deployment, provider accounts, logs, and retention.

Frontdesk's reviewed pages do not advertise self-hosting or public source access. LobbyStack customers can inspect the MIT-licensed code, choose the managed product, or deploy the stack on infrastructure they control. They keep a route out of vendor lock-in.

## A lead-generation agency example

An agency builds websites and paid search campaigns for ten home-service clients. It wants an AI receptionist that answers each client's calls and books jobs.

Frontdesk gives the agency a hosted omnichannel product and white-label partner plans from $500 a month. That can shorten launch time when the agency wants the vendor to own the platform.

LobbyStack gives the agency a lower-cost receptionist product, code it can inspect, and a stack it can deploy. The agency can control provider accounts, infrastructure, retention, and client-specific call logic instead of building those services around a closed platform.

The agency should test tenant separation, client access, provider billing, call review, calendar failures, and export before choosing. A white-label logo alone does not settle those operational questions.

## Choose Frontdesk when

Choose Frontdesk when you need a hosted voice, messaging, chat, email, and CRM bundle and plan to use those channels as one sales workspace. Its broader surface can replace several tools for a small team starting without a CRM, and its warm transfers suit teams that want the AI to hand callers to a colleague under set rules.

## Choose LobbyStack when

Choose LobbyStack to test voice conversations in your browser on a free plan that doesn't expire, pay $30 instead of $99 to start, or get 500 minutes for $100. On a paid plan, you can receive telephone calls on a dedicated number without replacing your CRM. No LobbyStack plan has a setup fee.
