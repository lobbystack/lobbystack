Test calls from your dashboard and the voice button in your website widget now run on GPT-Live, the voice model OpenAI built for ChatGPT Voice. Your receptionist listens while it speaks, stops when a caller cuts in, and keeps talking while it checks your calendar or takes a message. Phone numbers move to GPT-Live next. [Read how we made the switch](/blog/ai-voice-agent-gpt-live/).

Website chat now uses the same agent as your calls. A visitor can ask for an opening, book it, or leave a message for your team, and they get the same answers a caller would.

A new **Appointments** setting in your agent settings decides how your receptionist handles bookings. It can book appointments directly, take requests you confirm from the inbox, or take a message instead. Your receptionist keeps booking directly until you change it.

We also fixed a problem that stopped some new accounts from booking at all. Every business can now take appointments from its first call.
