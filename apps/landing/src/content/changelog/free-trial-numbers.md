New Free accounts in the US and Canada now get a real phone number for 14 days, with no card. Your receptionist answers calls on it, and you can forward your business line to it. During the trial, transfers go to the mobile number you verified. To keep the number after 14 days, choose Starter or Pro.

Setup now starts with a call. After you add your website and greeting, you call your receptionist from the browser, then verify your mobile number and pick your business number.

Your receptionist now speaks seven languages: English, French, Spanish, German, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian and Dutch. Calls open in the language you pick, and your receptionist switches when a caller asks for another one or speaks it. Website chat now answers in that language too.

Every plan answers as many calls at once as come in. The pricing page now says so on each plan.
