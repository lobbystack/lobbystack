The **Appointments** page now shows your bookings on a day, week or month calendar. Click an empty slot to book a customer at that time. LobbyStack checks availability and sends the same confirmation texts as when your agent books.

Each appointment has two kinds of notes. Your agent can read **Notes** to a caller once it has verified them. **Internal notes** stay with your team, and the agent, the customer and the API never see them.

Click **Time off** to close the whole business for a holiday, or block one employee's afternoon. Nobody can book that time, your agent included.
