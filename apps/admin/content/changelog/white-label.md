White Label lets agencies and resellers sell LobbyStack's AI receptionist to their clients under their own brand. Your clients sign in at your address, a subdomain of receptionistportal.com. On a paid tier, you can connect a subdomain of your own website, such as app.acmeagency.com. Clients see your name, logo, favicon and brand color, and their invitations and alerts carry your name.

You run every client from the partner console, which has pages for Businesses, Templates, Demos, Packages, Branding & domain, Team, API keys, Payments and Settings. Create a business for each client, from scratch or from a template of one you've set up, and invite its owner. On Demos, you build a receptionist from a prospect's website so they can talk to it before they buy. You can open any client's dashboard to help, and the business's audit log records who on your team opened it.

Each client pays you at a checkout in your company's name, through the payment account you connect on the **Payments** page. On **Packages**, you set each package's monthly price and its allowance of minutes and chats, with an optional setup fee. You can turn on overage for a client at your own rate per minute and per chat, up to a monthly cap.

You pay LobbyStack for a partner tier. Its pool of minutes and chats covers all your clients, however many businesses you add. The Free tier needs no card and comes with one 14-day trial number, and paid tiers include phone numbers. White Label has its own sign-up at [app.lobbystack.com/partner/signup](https://app.lobbystack.com/partner/signup?lng=en), and the [White Label page](/white-label/) compares the tiers. If you host LobbyStack yourself, the new [self-hosted pricing page](/pricing/self-hosted/) lists support plans.
