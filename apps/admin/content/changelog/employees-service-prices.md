You can now add your employees on the Employees page and choose the services each one does. Callers can ask for someone by name. Your receptionist books that person, or the first available person who does the service. Employees work your business hours unless you give them their own days and times. Add their time off, and your receptionist won't book them on those days and tells callers when they're back. If an employee has a phone number, your receptionist transfers callers who ask for them to it.

Services now have a price: a fixed price, a starting price, a range, or a price you set in person. Your receptionist quotes the price you entered, and when you leave it empty, it doesn't guess one. Pick your currency in AI settings.

Calls can now open in Serbian, which makes eight languages. Your receptionist still switches to the caller's language when they speak another one.
