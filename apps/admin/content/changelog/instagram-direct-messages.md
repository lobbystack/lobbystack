Open **Integrations**, click **Connect** on the Instagram card, and log in with your business's Instagram account. From then on, your AI receptionist answers the direct messages people send that account. It uses your knowledge, rules, services and employees, and it can book appointments in the chat.

The receptionist replies in the language each person writes in, and it stays quiet when someone only says "thanks" or "ok". Instagram conversations appear in **Messages** next to your texts and website chats. Reply in one, and you take it over from the receptionist.

You need an Instagram business or creator account. The [Instagram guide](https://docs.lobbystack.com/integrations/instagram) covers connecting, reconnecting and the 24-hour reply window.
