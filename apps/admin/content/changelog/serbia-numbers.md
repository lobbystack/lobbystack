Owners in Serbia can now give their agent a number. In the number step of setup, pick **RS** in the **Country** list and choose a landline number. You can start with it free for 14 days, as in the US and Canada.

Serbian numbers take calls. They don't send or receive texts.

On Starter, Pro and Enterprise, an extra Serbian number costs $12.00 a month.
