Owners in Serbia on a paid plan can now give their agent a number. In the number step of setup, pick **RS** in the **Country** list and choose a city. Serbian numbers have no free trial.

Activation can take up to 20 business days while the number is checked. You can keep setting up your agent meanwhile, and the number joins your workspace when it goes live.

Serbian numbers take calls. They don't send or receive texts.

On Starter, Pro and Enterprise, an extra Serbian number costs $12.00 a month.
