You can now connect Calendly from **Integrations**. Pick an event type, and each appointment your AI receptionist books appears in Calendly as a booking of that type. Your other Calendly events, and the calendars Calendly checks, block those times for callers.

Changes you make in Calendly come back to LobbyStack. Cancel a booking there and LobbyStack cancels the appointment. Move one and LobbyStack moves the appointment and its reminder. You get an alert either way, and you tell the caller about a new time.

Calendly needs a paid plan, Standard or above, and caps bookings from other apps at 100 a day per user below Enterprise. It gets the caller's name and a stand-in email address, so it doesn't email callers. To connect your account, start from the [integrations guide](https://docs.lobbystack.com/integrations/overview).
