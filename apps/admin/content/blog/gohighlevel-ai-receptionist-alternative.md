A **GoHighLevel AI receptionist** runs on HighLevel's Voice AI, so it suits agencies whose clients already live in that CRM. [LobbyStack](/white-label/) suits agencies that want to sell only a receptionist, with a free partner tier, one shared pool of minutes and MIT-licensed code.

We read HighLevel's [pricing page](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing) and its [AI pricing article](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing) on November 1, 2026. LobbyStack sells its own program, so check the sources below.

## GoHighLevel is a full agency platform

HighLevel gives an agency a CRM, pipelines, campaigns, funnels and sub-accounts for each client. Its [pricing page](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing) lists Starter at $97 a month with 3 sub-accounts, Unlimited at $297 with unlimited sub-accounts, and Founder at $497. The page says SaaS Mode, on that top plan, "transforms HighLevel into your own sellable software product."

Voice AI sits inside that account. An agent can [update a contact field or trigger a workflow](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000005267-separate-during-call-and-post-call-actions-in-voice-ai) during a call, and that native link is the main reason to keep the receptionist in HighLevel. If you already sell the suite, adding a receptionist there costs you little setup and no new login for your client.

## How does GoHighLevel Voice AI pricing work?

HighLevel's [AI pricing article](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing) splits the bill into parts. On pay-per-use, the Voice Engine costs $0.045 a minute. A voice adds to that: $0.015 a minute for OpenAI TTS-1, and $0.035 for ElevenLabs Flash. LLM tokens and phone system charges come on top. The same article prices OpenAI GPT Live 1 as a speech-to-speech option at $0.12 a minute.

AI Employee plans apply per sub-account: Growth costs $50 a month with 100 AI Agent minutes, and Unlimited costs $97 with unlimited use subject to fair use. Starter, at $20, includes no Voice AI minutes and is open only to agencies on the $497 plan.

You choose how to pass this on. HighLevel's [rebilling article](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000008422-fixed-rate-rebilling-for-ai-products) describes a markup on token cost, or a fixed rate per unit. The agency still pays HighLevel for actual use, so a fixed rate that sits too low costs you the difference.

## How does the cost compare for ten clients?

Take ten clients who use 100 minutes each, or 1,000 minutes a month, on a suite you already pay for.

| 1,000 minutes a month | Monthly cost | Not included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HighLevel, $0.045 engine and $0.015 voice | $60 on top of your plan | LLM tokens and phone charges |
| HighLevel, GPT Live 1 | $120 on top of your plan | Phone charges |
| HighLevel, AI Employee Unlimited on each account | $970 on top of your plan | Phone charges |
| LobbyStack Growth tier | $199 | Nothing else at this volume, with 10 numbers and 1,300 minutes |

If your agency already pays $297 for HighLevel, its per-minute route costs less on paper. If you'd buy HighLevel only to sell a receptionist, LobbyStack's Growth tier replaces that plan and costs one flat amount, which makes your margin easier to predict.

## What does LobbyStack do differently?

**You sell one product.** Your client gets a receptionist that answers calls, books appointments, takes messages and transfers urgent calls. They log in to a small dashboard under your brand, and you skip the CRM training.

**You can start at $0.** The Free tier needs no card and includes 30 voice minutes, enough to send prospects a [demo built from their own website](https://docs.lobbystack.com/white-label/demos). Direct customers also get a free plan with 30 voice minutes and a US or Canadian number free for 14 days.

**You keep the payments.** Clients pay at a checkout in your company's name, into your Stripe account, and LobbyStack takes no share. Our guide to [starting an AI receptionist business](/blog/start-ai-receptionist-business/) walks through packages and pricing.

**You can read the code.** The [repository](https://github.com/lobbystack/lobbystack) is MIT licensed. A [Railway template](https://docs.lobbystack.com/self-hosting/overview) deploys it with your own OpenAI and Twilio credentials, and you take on hosting and upgrades.

**You can run it from chat.** Connect Claude or ChatGPT to your partner through [LobbyStack's MCP server](https://docs.lobbystack.com/white-label/mcp-for-agencies), and ask it to make a demo or create a package.

LobbyStack has limits an agency should weigh. It has no CRM, funnels or campaigns, and no native HighLevel connection, so you'd send data across with webhooks and the API. It answers inbound calls only. It doesn't reply to inbound texts with AI, and website chat isn't part of hosted plans. Numbers come from the United States, Canada and Puerto Rico, voices come from GPT-Live, and it doesn't clone voices.

## Moving a receptionist from GoHighLevel

You can't import a Voice AI agent. Plan it as a short rebuild for each client:

1. Copy the agent's prompt into LobbyStack's rules, and its facts into knowledge entries.
2. Add the client's website, so the receptionist reads the services and hours.
3. Add the services the client books, and connect Google Calendar or Cal.com.
4. Save the result as a [template](https://docs.lobbystack.com/white-label/templates) for the next client in that trade.
5. Forward the client's line to the new number, and keep the old agent until you've heard real calls.

## Choose GoHighLevel when

Choose GoHighLevel when your clients already run their CRM, campaigns and follow-up there, and you want the receptionist to act on those records. It also fits agencies that sell the full suite through SaaS Mode, or that want to pick from many voices and models.

## Choose LobbyStack when

Choose LobbyStack when a receptionist is the whole offer, when your clients don't need a CRM, or when you want to test the business at $0 before you commit to a monthly plan. It also fits agencies that want the source code as an exit, with a Railway deploy on their own accounts.
