## What is an outsourced phone answering service?

An outsourced phone answering service is a team of remote operators who answer your calls in your company's name. You forward your line to the provider, all the time or only when you don't pick up. The operator takes a message, transfers the call to you or, on some plans, books an appointment in your calendar.

French providers use three names for related services:

- **Standard externalisé** (outsourced switchboard): operators screen calls and transfer them to your team. Optilib sells it as a separate plan.
- **Permanence téléphonique**: the operator's main job is taking detailed messages and passing them on. Absys draws this line on its own page.
- **Télésecrétariat** or **secrétariat téléphonique** (remote secretary): messages and appointments, common with independent professionals and tradespeople.

Our guide to [what a phone answering service is](/blog/what-is-a-phone-answering-service/) explains these formats. The prices below cover all three, so check what each plan does with a call before you compare amounts.

## Per-call pricing or a bundle: which costs less?

At low volume, a small monthly fee plus a price per call costs the least. Op'tel Services (€15 plus €1.25 a call) costs less than Le Secrétariat's €50 minimum below 28 calls a month. Above that, Le Secrétariat's €1 per call wins. A bundle like Optilib's fixes your budget as long as you stay under the included calls.

Four things change the real bill:

- **VAT and your tax status.** A business that recovers VAT pays the pre-tax price. A micro-entrepreneur under the VAT exemption pays VAT on top, 20% more: Op'tel Services' €140 for 100 calls becomes €168.
- **How long a call can run.** Thelem includes 3 minutes per call and then charges €0.89 a minute. The other pages set no limit.
- **The price of extra calls.** Optilib charges for calls beyond the bundle but doesn't publish the rate, so a busy month can go past the listed €59 or €139.
- **Add-ons.** Thelem charges €15 for booking into Google Calendar and €21 with a text reminder, without saying whether that's monthly.

## What hours does outsourced phone answering cover?

The published plans cover weekdays and Saturday morning. Op'tel Services answers Monday to Friday from 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. and Saturday from 8 a.m. to noon, 59 hours a week. Optilib covers Monday to Saturday from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., and Thelem includes an extension to 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., Monday to Saturday. Only Bureau24's Professionnel plan advertises 24/7 service.

Evenings, Sundays and public holidays fall outside those hours. If your customers call about a leak on a Sunday, ask who answers at those hours and what it costs, or cover those hours with an [after-hours answering service](/solutions/after-hours-answering-service/).

## How do you choose an outsourced phone answering provider?

1. **Count your calls.** From your line's call history, note last month's call count, the average length and the share that came in after hours.
2. **Mark the calls that need a person.** An urgent quote, an unhappy customer or a negotiation needs a human. A question about hours or a booking doesn't.
3. **Price three bills.** Run your volume through a per-call plan, a fee-plus-calls plan and a bundle, using the tables above.
4. **Get the full quote in writing.** Ask for the extra-call price, the minutes included per call, the hours covered, the price with and without VAT, and the notice period.
5. **Test with real calls.** Le Secrétariat and Bureau24 offer a free trial. Call yourself at 12:30 p.m. and 7:45 p.m. to see who answers.
6. **Forward your line.** All of these services work through call forwarding from your carrier. Our guide on [how to forward business calls](/blog/how-to-forward-business-calls/) explains the types: all calls, when busy, or when unanswered.

## Questions about outsourced phone answering in France

### How much does a remote phone secretary cost in France?

In October 2026, providers that publish prices charge €1 to €1.99 per call before VAT, with a monthly fee of €0 to €39 or a billing minimum. At 40 calls a month, the bill runs from €50 to €118.60. Bundles start around €59 a month. Ask each provider for the price of calls beyond the bundle.

### Can I keep my business number?

Yes. You set up call forwarding with your carrier to the number the provider gives you: all calls, or only the ones you don't answer. Your customers dial the same number as before. LobbyStack works the same way, except that the forwarding goes to a number in the US, Canada, the UK, Australia or New Zealand.

### Do outsourced answering services in France require a contract?

Not at most providers that publish prices. Op'tel Services, Le Secrétariat, Kandbaz and Optilib's remote secretary plan state no commitment. Thelem says most of its contracts have no commitment or notice period, and Bureau24 has no long-term commitment. Get the notice period in writing before you sign, because the pricing pages don't spell it out.

### Do they answer at night and on weekends?

The published entry plans stop at Saturday noon, or at 10 p.m. at Thelem. Bureau24 advertises 24/7 answering on its Professionnel plan, from €189.90 a month. For nights and Sundays, plan on an AI receptionist or forwarding to the on-call person's mobile. Ask each provider where calls go outside its hours before you sign up.
