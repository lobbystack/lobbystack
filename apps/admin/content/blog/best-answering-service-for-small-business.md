## How do live, AI and hybrid answering services differ?

A live service puts a person on the call and bills by the minute or the call. An AI service answers with software and bills by the minute, the call or the unique caller. A hybrid answers with AI and hands calls to a person. Entry prices start at $29 plus $1.15 a minute for live (AnswerNet) and $14 for 75 minutes for AI (AI-Receptionist.com). For hybrid, Moneypenny's AI plans hand calls to its people from $99 for 50 calls, and Smith.ai's AI starts free.

**Live answering services** suit calls that need judgment: an upset customer, a long intake, a caller who wants a person. You pay for that time, and live plans in this list cost $127 to $485 at 40 calls.

**AI answering services** cost less at the same volume, from $25.25 to $99 at 40 calls for the AI and hybrid plans here. LobbyStack Starter costs $30 for 150 minutes, and its plans don't limit how many calls it answers at once. The AI follows the rules you write, so decide when it should transfer a call or take a message.

**Hybrid services** come from companies that run both AI and live receptionists, so an upset caller or an unusual question can still reach a person. Smith.ai and Moneypenny sell per-call AI plans that hand calls to their people, and Abby Connect draws human and AI minutes from one plan.

## How to choose an answering service for your small business

Decide first who must answer: a person, AI, or AI with a person behind it. Then check these points on each vendor's own pages. Trades that dispatch technicians after hours should also read our list of the [best HVAC answering services](/blog/best-hvac-answering-services/).

### Match the billing unit to your call length

Per-minute plans favor short calls, per-call plans favor long ones (Smith.ai, Upfirst), and per-caller plans favor repeat callers (Goodcall). Rounding changes the bill too. SAS, Davinci Virtual and LobbyStack bill by the second, and PATLive in 6-second steps after a one-minute minimum. Abby Connect and Ambs Call Center round to 30 seconds, Moneypenny to 15 seconds, Ruby to 60 seconds, and MAP Communications, The Perfect Answer and Answering Service Care to whole minutes. With 60-second rounding, a 40-second call costs a full minute.

### Check coverage hours and languages

Ruby, PATLive and AnswerFirst charge nothing extra for nights, weekends or holidays, and Abby Connect has no weekend upcharge. Davinci Virtual's FAQ puts its live team on weekdays from 8am to 8pm Eastern, while its live-receptionists page says 24/7, so ask before you count on nights. Most live services here answer in English and Spanish. Among AI services, Upfirst lists 35+ languages and AI-Receptionist.com 55+, and LobbyStack switches to the caller's language across 70+.

### Ask what booking, transfers and texts cost

Smith.ai's human receptionists charge $1.50 a call to book, and Davinci Virtual books only on Premium plans from $249. SAS charges $0.10 a minute and Ambs Call Center $0.06 a minute while a caller is patched to you. PATLive charges $50 once to turn on texting. LobbyStack texts booking confirmations and reminders to US and Canadian numbers, but its AI doesn't reply to texts.

### Check the setup fee

Davinci Virtual charges $95 to set up live answering, Ambs Call Center $85 and Answering Service Care $50, and AnswerConnect's 2025 price sheet lists $49.99. Moneypenny and Nexa charge a setup fee without publishing the amount. Most others state none, and LobbyStack has none on any plan.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which answering services answer in Spanish?

Ruby, PATLive and Abby Connect include Spanish-speaking receptionists at no extra cost, and Moneypenny, MAP Communications, The Perfect Answer and Ambs Call Center list Spanish or bilingual answering on every plan. Smith.ai charges $1 a call for a Spanish line, Answering Service Care charges $1 a minute for live translation, and AnswerConnect's 2025 price sheet lists Spanish as an add-on. ### Can I keep my business phone number?

Yes. With most services, you set your carrier to forward calls to a number the service gives you, so callers keep dialing the number they know. Upfirst also ports numbers at no extra charge. With LobbyStack, you forward your existing number to your LobbyStack number, or contact the team if you'd rather port it.

### Do answering services require a contract?

Most don't. PATLive, SAS, Smith.ai and Abby Connect run month to month. Davinci Virtual needs 30 days' written notice to cancel, Moneypenny 30 days after the 3-month first term in its terms, and Smith.ai's AI plans 30 days. LobbyStack lets you cancel anytime from billing settings, with no cancellation fees.

### Can an answering service book appointments?

Yes, though the price varies. Smith.ai's human receptionists charge $1.50 per booked call, Davinci Virtual books only on Premium plans from $249, and Rosie books into a calendar from $149. Upfirst, AI-Receptionist.com and LobbyStack book on their cheapest paid plans. LobbyStack books into Google Calendar, Cal.com or Calendly, the only calendars it connects to.

### Do answering services charge for spam calls?

Some don't. Smith.ai's receptionists don't bill spam when your lines pass Caller ID, and its AI filters known spammers before answering. Ruby doesn't count robocalls its optional filter sends to voicemail, and The Perfect Answer, Upfirst and Goodcall leave spam off the bill. LobbyStack doesn't count calls under 10 seconds or calls its receptionist ends as spam. Ambs Call Center counts solicitor calls against your minutes.

### Is there a free answering service for small businesses?

No live service in this list is free. Smith.ai's AI Free plan answers 25 real calls a month at $0, then charges $3 a call. LobbyStack Free gives you 30 voice minutes a month and a US or Canadian number free for 14 days, with no card, and Goodcall offers a free demo agent. Our guide to [AI receptionist cost, including free plans and trials](/blog/ai-receptionist-savings/) lists the rest.
