An **AI phone assistant** is software that answers your business calls in a natural voice. It answers questions from your information, books appointments into your calendar, transfers urgent calls to a person and sends you a summary of each call. You can set one up in French in an evening, then test it before a customer ever calls.

*LobbyStack wrote this guide and sells an AI phone assistant. The steps follow its dashboard, and the same questions apply to other tools.*

## How does an AI phone assistant work?

You forward your line to the assistant, or give it a number of its own. When a customer calls:

1. The assistant picks up in your business's default language, with the greeting you wrote.
2. It listens to the request and answers from your website, your documents and your notes.
3. It acts on your rules: it books an open time, rings your cell for an emergency or takes a message.
4. After the call, the summary, transcript and recording appear in your dashboard.

You'll also see it called an AI voice agent, an AI receptionist or an AI answering machine. With voicemail, the caller waits for your callback. With an AI assistant, they hang up with an answer or an appointment.

### What it doesn't do

An AI phone assistant knows only what you give it. If your prices change and nobody updates them, it quotes the old ones. It also doesn't replace a person for an angry customer or a request no rule covers. Plan a transfer or a message for those calls.

## How do you set up an AI phone assistant in French?

The six steps below follow LobbyStack. Give it an evening.

### 1. Make French the default language

In the AI settings, the **default customer language** decides the language each call opens in. Choose French. When a caller asks for English or clearly speaks another language, LobbyStack switches for the rest of the call. The **team language** decides the language of the summaries and messages you receive.

In Quebec this choice matters: since Bill 96, article 50.2 of the [Charter of the French Language](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/lc/C-11) requires a business serving consumers to respect their right to be served in French. Our comparison of [phone answering services in Quebec](/blog/phone-answering-service-quebec/) explains what the article means for your line.

### 2. Give it your website and documents

Paste your website address, and LobbyStack reads the pages and answers from them. Then add what your site doesn't say: your prices, hours, service area and cancellation policies, as text or PDFs. Write them in French, in your customers' words.

### 3. Write your greeting

Keep it short: the business name and a question. For example: "Plomberie Gagnon, bonjour ! Comment puis-je vous aider ?" The caller should know within two seconds that they reached the right business.

### 4. Connect your calendar

Connect Google Calendar in the integrations and pick the calendar for bookings. The assistant then offers open times during the call and books. If you'd rather approve each appointment, request mode saves the time the caller wants and your team confirms it. Customers who agree get a confirmation text.

### 5. Write your rules in plain words

A rule tells the assistant what to do on one kind of call. For example:

- "If someone has an active water leak, ask for the address and transfer to the on-call cell."
- "If the caller wants a quote for a bathroom, ask for the town and book a visit."
- "For a billing question, take a message for Julie."

Enter the default transfer number, the one that rings for emergencies. If nobody picks up, LobbyStack tells the caller and offers to take a message.

### 6. Test it, then forward your line

Call the assistant from your browser and play your hardest calls: a customer in a hurry, a price question, an emergency, an English-speaking caller. Read the transcript and fix your notes. When you like the answers, forward your line on busy, on no answer, after hours or all the time. ## Questions about AI phone assistants

### Does an AI phone assistant speak French?

Yes, if the product supports it. LobbyStack opens the call in French when that's your default language and answers in more than 70 languages with OpenAI's GPT-Live. We don't promise a Quebec accent, so listen to it with your own expressions before you forward your line.

### What's the difference between an AI phone assistant and an AI voice agent?

In practice, none. Both terms mean software that answers the phone with a voice. People say voice agent when it takes actions, such as booking or transferring, and assistant when it answers questions, but most tools do both.

### Can I keep my current number?

Yes. You turn on call forwarding with your phone provider to the assistant's number. Your customers dial the same number as before.
