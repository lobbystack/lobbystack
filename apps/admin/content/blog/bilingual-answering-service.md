In November 2026, live services such as PATLive, Specialty Answering Service and Ruby cost $188 to $485 for 40 calls a month, and several limit Spanish to daytime hours. ## Which bilingual answering service is best?

Pick by two questions: when do your Spanish-speaking customers call, and do they need a person or just a fast, accurate answer? Live services put a fluent person on the line, but some only staff Spanish during the day. AI receptionists answer in Spanish around the clock and cost less per call.

- **Ruby, best for Spanish with a person at any hour:** live Spanish and bilingual call handling 24/7, from $250 a month for 50 minutes.
- **PATLive, best published rate card:** Spanish-speaking receptionists from 8am to 10pm Eastern at no extra cost, from $49 a month plus $2.99 a minute.
- **Specialty Answering Service, best low-cost live service:** a bilingual team, per-second billing and a 14-day trial, from $44 a month plus $1.54 a minute.
- **Posh, best for weekday Spanish coverage:** bilingual receptionists Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm Eastern, from $130 a month for 50 minutes.
- **Smith.ai receptionists, best for intake-heavy calls:** 24/7 live agents in English or Spanish, billed per call, plus $1.00 a call for a dedicated Spanish line.
- **Upfirst, best low-cost AI for many languages:** 35+ languages with transcripts translated into yours, from $24.95 a month for 30 calls.
- **LobbyStack (ours), best for callers who speak more than Spanish:** 70+ languages, booking and transfers for $30 a month for 150 minutes.

## The best live bilingual answering services

A live service puts a person on every call. That person can calm an upset caller, follow a heavy accent and use judgment on an odd request. You pay for it by the minute or by the call, and the Spanish hours can be shorter than the English ones.

### Ruby: best for Spanish with a person at any hour

Ruby's plans differ only in minutes: $250 for 50, $395 for 100, $720 for 200 and $1,725 for 500 ([pricing page](https://www.ruby.com/plans-and-pricing/)). The pricing page lists 24/7 Spanish and bilingual call handling among its features, and its [bilingual page](https://www.ruby.com/bilingual-answering-service/) says receptionists answer in English and Spanish 24/7/365 and relay each message back to you in English. Ruby's [help center](https://rubyhelpcenter.helpjuice.com/billing-plans/am-i-on-the-right-billing-plan) lists extra minutes at $4.50 on the 100-minute plan and $4.40 on the 200-minute plan. Ruby is the most expensive option here at both volumes.

### PATLive: best published rate card

PATLive's [pricing page](https://www.patlive.com/pricing/) lists 11 tiers, from $49 a month plus $2.99 a minute up to 5,000 minutes, with no contract and a 14-day trial. Its [bilingual page](https://www.patlive.com/solutions/bilingual-receptionists/) puts Spanish-speaking receptionists on your calls from 8am to 10pm Eastern, 365 days a year, with the same capabilities as its English service and no extra cost. English runs 24/7, and the page says every receptionist is a person in the US, with no AI. Ask PATLive what happens to a Spanish call after 10pm Eastern, when only English hours remain.

### Specialty Answering Service: best low-cost live service

SAS costs $44 a month plus $1.54 a minute on its Economy plan, or $159 for 100 minutes and $269 for 220, each with $1.44 extra minutes ([pricing page](https://www.specialtyansweringservice.net/pricing/)). There's no setup fee, and patching a caller through to you costs $0.10 a minute. Its [bilingual page](https://www.specialtyansweringservice.net/answering-services/international/bilingual-answering-service/) says Spanish-speaking staff take messages, schedule appointments and answer customer service questions, and that its team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The page doesn't give separate Spanish hours, so confirm overnight Spanish coverage during the 14-day trial.

### Posh: best for weekday Spanish coverage

Posh charges $130 for 50 minutes, $215 for 100 and $420 for 200, with extra minutes from $2.20 down to $1.90 ([pricing page](https://posh.com/pricing)). Its [bilingual page](https://posh.com/services/bilingual-answering-service/) says Spanish-speaking receptionists answer Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm Eastern, and make outbound calls in Spanish. Outside those hours, Spanish calls go to a Spanish voicemail greeting or to an English-speaking receptionist. That fits an office that takes Spanish calls during business hours and closes at night.

### Smith.ai receptionists: best for intake-heavy calls

Smith.ai's live receptionists bill per call: $300 for 30 calls, $810 for 90 and $2,100 for 300, with overage from $11.50 to $8.50 a call ([pricing page](https://smith.ai/pricing/receptionists)). A dedicated Spanish line costs $1.00 a call, and every call on it goes to a receptionist fluent in English and Spanish. Booking costs $1.50 a call. Its [bilingual page](https://smith.ai/features/bilingual-answering-service) describes 24/7 coverage by North America-based agents in either language, with call recordings and transcripts in both. Per-call billing makes long Spanish intake calls cheaper here than at a per-minute service.

### Abby Connect: people and AI on one plan

Abby Connect sells 50 Abby Minutes for $164, 100 for $329, 200 for $599 and 500 for $1,380 ([pricing page](https://www.abby.com/pricing/)). The page lists English and Spanish answering among its features, no weekend upcharge and a free trial, and says choosing AI doubles your Abby Minutes. It doesn't list an overage rate or Spanish hours, so ask for both before you compare it with the others.

## AI receptionists that answer in Spanish and other languages

An AI receptionist answers in the caller's language at 2pm or 2am for the same price. The good ones switch mid-call when the caller does, and many cover languages no live service here offers. You pay a flat plan, so a Spanish call costs the same as an English one. The trade-off: when a caller insists on a person, the AI transfers them to your team, so someone there needs to speak Spanish or take a callback.

### Upfirst: best low-cost AI for many languages

Upfirst bills per call: $24.95 a month for 30 calls, $59.95 for 90, $159.95 for 300 and $299 for 600, with extra calls from $1.50 down to $0.70 ([pricing page](https://upfirst.ai/pricing)). Every plan includes 35+ languages, and the pricing page says the receptionist switches languages automatically, then translates the transcript into yours. Its [bilingual page](https://upfirst.ai/solutions/bilingual) covers booking, messages and lead questions in English and Spanish. Warm transfers and two-way texting come on every plan, and the trial runs 14 days with no card.

### Smith.ai AI Receptionist: best free start for Spanish

Smith.ai's [AI Receptionist](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist) is free for 25 real calls a month, then $3.00 a call. Pro costs $150 for 75 calls, then $2.50 a call. Its [bilingual page](https://smith.ai/features/bilingual-answering-service) says that with Spanish turned on, the AI detects whether the caller speaks English or Spanish and continues in that language, and it can hand a call to a bilingual human agent for an extra fee. The free plan needs a card at signup.

### Rosie: Spanish on every plan

Rosie costs $49 a month for 250 minutes, $149 for 1,000 and $299 for 2,000, with a 7-day trial ([pricing page](https://heyrosie.com/pricing)). The page lists English and Spanish on every call. The $49 plan takes messages and texts callers a booking link. Booking into your calendar, warm transfers and live transfers start on the $149 plan, which is why the table shows two prices at 40 calls.

### My AI Front Desk: AI plus texting and a CRM

My AI Front Desk costs $99 a month, or $79 billed annually, for 200 voice minutes, web chat, two-way texting and a built-in CRM ([pricing page](https://www.myaifrontdesk.com/pricing)). The page lists 20+ languages and a 7-day trial. Past 200 minutes, a voice minute costs 25 credits, or $0.25. The plan also includes a monthly credit allowance that can lower that, which is why the table says "up to $124" at 100 calls.

## What happens to a Spanish call outside Spanish hours?

At PATLive and Posh, a Spanish call outside Spanish hours still gets answered, but by an English-speaking receptionist or a Spanish voicemail greeting. For a business whose Spanish-speaking customers call at night, such as a plumber or a locksmith, that gap matters more than price.

- **Spanish around the clock with a person:** Ruby and Smith.ai say 24/7. SAS describes a 24/7 bilingual team without separate Spanish hours, so confirm it.
- **Spanish in the daytime only:** PATLive covers 8am to 10pm Eastern every day, and Posh covers 8am to 6pm Eastern on weekdays. Both times are Eastern, so a Pacific business loses Spanish coverage three hours earlier on its own clock.
- **Spanish at any hour, no person:** every AI receptionist here answers in Spanish 24/7.

Many owners mix the two: a live service during the day, and an AI receptionist on nights and weekends. Your carrier's call forwarding decides which one rings. [How to forward business calls](/blog/how-to-forward-business-calls/) lists the codes for each carrier.

## How do you test a bilingual answering service?

### 1. Count your Spanish calls by hour

Check your call log or ask your team when Spanish-speaking customers call. If most come after 6pm, Posh's weekday hours won't help and PATLive stops at 10pm Eastern.

### 2. Write your script in both languages, or let the service translate it

Live services work from the call instructions you give them. Ask whether a person translates your script or you supply the Spanish version. AI receptionists follow one set of instructions in every language. Our [phone answering scripts](/blog/phone-answering-script/) give you a starting point.

### 3. Place three test calls in Spanish

Call as a new customer asking for a quote, as an existing customer moving an appointment, and as someone who starts in English and switches to Spanish halfway. Spell a name and an address. Then check what the message said, in which language, and whether the booking landed at the right time.

### 4. Keep your business number

Ask each finalist whether it answers through call forwarding from your existing number. LobbyStack does, so your customers keep dialing the number on your truck, website and Google profile.

The five live services in this list name only English and Spanish on the pages we checked. AI receptionists go further: My AI Front Desk lists 20+ languages, Upfirst 35+ and LobbyStack 70+.

### Can an AI receptionist switch languages in the middle of a call?

Yes. Upfirst says its receptionist switches languages automatically, and Smith.ai says its AI detects English or Spanish and continues in that language. LobbyStack switches when a caller asks or clearly speaks another language. Test it by starting a call in English and switching to Spanish.
