A **virtual secretary** works remotely for your business: they answer your calls, handle your email and calendar, and take on part of your admin. In Quebec, the placement agencies that publish prices asked $9.99 to $19.95 an hour in November 2026, plus recruiting fees. If all you need is someone to answer the phone, an AI receptionist costs less.

## What does a virtual secretary do?

A virtual secretary, also called a virtual assistant ("adjointe virtuelle" in Quebec), does an office secretary's work from a distance. Depending on the agreement, they:

- answer calls in your company's name and take messages;
- manage your calendar and confirm appointments;
- sort your email and reply to routine requests;
- prepare quotes, invoices and payment reminders;
- keep your client files up to date.

They work the hours you buy. A call at 9 p.m. or during their break goes to your voicemail, unless you add another service.

### Virtual secretary, virtual receptionist or AI receptionist?

The three terms overlap, but they sell different things:

| | Virtual secretary | Virtual receptionist | AI receptionist |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who answers | A person, often on their own | A team of agents | Software |
| Work | Calls and admin | Calls only | Calls only |
| Hours | The hours you buy | Often 24/7 | 24/7 |
| Billing | By the hour | Per call or per minute, by quote | Per minute or per call |

## How much does a virtual secretary cost in Quebec?

Few freelancers publish their rates. The two placement agencies for Quebec we found with prices online showed this on November 3, 2026:

| Agency | Published hourly rate | Other published fees |
|---|---|---|
| [Adjointe Virtuelle Propulsion](https://www.adjointevirtuellepropulsion.net/plans-tarifs) (Quebec City) | $15.35 to $19.95 an hour, by plan | Recruiting $588, onboarding from $688 |
| [AV du Nord](https://web.archive.org/web/20251113110512/https://avdunord.ca/adjointe-virtuelle-tarif-comparatif-au-quebec/) | From $9.99 an hour | Not listed on the page |

These agencies place an assistant who works for you part-time. A freelancer sets their own rate and sometimes bills a minimum number of hours a month. Always ask for a written price, with taxes.

### The monthly cost

For an assistant 20 hours a week, about 87 hours a month:

- at $9.99 an hour: about $866 a month;
- at $15.35 an hour: about $1,330 a month, plus $588 for recruiting and at least $688 for onboarding the first time.

For a business that mostly needs someone to answer while it's on a job site, that's expensive. You pay for the hours when the phone doesn't ring.

## How do you choose between a virtual secretary and an AI receptionist?

1. **List your tasks.** Separate calls from everything else: email, invoices, files. If calls dominate, start with AI.
2. **Look at when calls come in.** If you mostly miss calls in the evening and on weekends, a daytime assistant won't take them.
3. **Count your minutes.** Multiply last month's calls by their average length. Compare that total with an AI receptionist's plan and with an assistant's hours.
4. **Check the French.** An assistant needs to write your email and invoices in French. An AI receptionist needs to open the call in French. Test it with your own expressions.
5. **Combine them if you need to.** The AI takes the calls and books, and the assistant handles messages and admin a few hours a week.

## Questions about virtual secretaries in Quebec

### What is a virtual secretary's hourly rate in Quebec?

### Can a virtual secretary answer my calls at night?

Only if you buy those hours. Most work days. For evening and weekend calls, add a phone answering service or an AI receptionist, which answers at any hour.

### Can a virtual secretary book my appointments?

Yes, if you give them access to your calendar. An AI receptionist like LobbyStack also books during the call, into Google Calendar, Cal.com or Calendly, and texts a confirmation to customers who agree.

### Can an AI receptionist replace a virtual secretary?

For routine calls, yes. For email, invoices and files, no. Many businesses keep an assistant for a few hours a week and let the AI answer the phone.
