# Frontdesk comparison research

Checked: 2026-07-30

- Product: https://www.myaifrontdesk.com/ai-receptionist

- White label: https://www.myaifrontdesk.com/white-label

## Publishable facts

Frontdesk, formerly My AI Front Desk, positions itself as an omnichannel AI workforce. The product spans voice, SMS, web chat, email, forms, CRM records, and automated lead sequences. Public pricing lists a $20 Basic plan without voice minutes and a $99 Business-in-a-Box plan with 200 voice minutes. Its annual equivalent is advertised at $79 per month. Enterprise pricing is custom.

The $99 plan advertises inbound and outbound voice, two-way SMS, booking, transfers, more than 20 languages, a native CRM, and Zapier. Public limits include 20 knowledge pages, the last 200 interaction logs, three notification recipients, and two editor seats.

## Fair comparison

Frontdesk has broader front-office channel coverage than LobbyStack. LobbyStack is a stronger fit for buyers who want a focused receptionist product with inspectable code and a supported self-hosting route.

## Claims to avoid

Use “Frontdesk (formerly My AI Front Desk)” in copy. Keep the former name in SEO metadata because buyers still search for it.
