## Publishable facts

RingCentral AI Receptionist, or AIR, is available as a standalone product and as an add-on in RingCentral's communications system. Public material lists standalone pricing from $49 per month with 100 minutes and RingEX add-on pricing from $39.

The product advertises FAQ answers, contextual routing, multilingual voice, analytics, transcripts, SMS, WhatsApp, Shopify, Calendly, booking, and call-queue overflow.

## Fair comparison

AIR fits teams already using RingCentral or buyers who want one large communications vendor. LobbyStack suits teams that value public code, self-hosting, and provider choice. Compare the complete phone-system relationship rather than the AI add-on price alone.

## Claims to avoid

Do not describe RingCentral as lacking integrations or enterprise administration.
