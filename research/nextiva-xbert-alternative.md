# Nextiva XBert comparison research

Checked: 2026-07-30

## Official sources

- Product and pricing: https://www.nextiva.com/products/xbert
- AI receptionist overview: https://www.nextiva.com/products/ai-receptionist

## Publishable facts

Nextiva positions XBert as an AI receptionist and customer-service assistant for phone, SMS, and web chat. Public pricing lists $99 per month for 100 interactions, followed by $0.99 per interaction. Voice, text, and chat use the shared interaction allowance.

Official pages advertise FAQs, qualification, booking and rescheduling, routing, transcripts, analytics, CRM and calendar integrations, multiple locations, and assisted setup. Nextiva describes XBert as an addition to a Nextiva plan.

## Fair comparison

XBert appeals to teams that want AI inside Nextiva's broader customer-experience suite. LobbyStack offers a focused receptionist stack, public code, and self-hosting. Buyers should price the required Nextiva plan and XBert together.

## Claims to avoid

Do not equate interactions with minutes or calls. Explain the unit before comparing prices.
