## Publishable facts

Goodcall prices each AI agent by unique callers. Public plans list $79 for 100 unique callers, $129 for 250, and $249 for 500, followed by $0.50 per caller. The plans advertise unlimited minutes and tokens, configurable skills, forms and logic flows, routing, transfers, call records, Zapier, and small-business integrations.

The Starter plan publicly lists one logic flow, three directory contacts, and seven days of call and customer history. Separate locations or distinct agents can require separate subscriptions.

## Fair comparison

Goodcall's unique-caller model can work well for long or repeated calls from the same customers. LobbyStack's minute model can fit businesses with many distinct short inquiries. LobbyStack also publishes its source and supports self-hosting.

## Claims to avoid

Do not call one billing unit cheaper without applying the buyer's real call mix.
