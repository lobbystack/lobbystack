# Rosie comparison research

Checked: 2026-07-30

## Official sources

- Product: https://heyrosie.com/
- Pricing: https://heyrosie.com/pricing

## Publishable facts

Rosie targets local and service businesses with a simple AI answering service. Public pricing lists $49 for 250 minutes, $149 for 1,000 minutes, and $299 for 2,000 minutes. The product advertises business knowledge, recordings, transcripts, summaries, spam detection, mobile apps, website chat, Zapier, English, and Spanish.

The $149 Scale plan adds calendar booking, live and warm transfers, and in-call texting. The $49 plan lists two message-taking scenarios.

## Fair comparison

Rosie offers generous public minute allowances. Buyers who only need answering and message capture may like its entry plan. Buyers who need booking or transfers should compare the Scale plan with LobbyStack's included workflows, public code, and deployment choices.

## Claims to avoid

Do not imply that the entry plan includes every feature shown elsewhere on Rosie's site.
