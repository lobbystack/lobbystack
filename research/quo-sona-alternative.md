# Quo Sona comparison research

Checked: 2026-07-30

## Official sources

- Product: https://www.quo.com/sona
- Pricing and allowance behavior: https://support.quo.com/core-concepts/ai-automations/sona/sona-pricing

## Publishable facts

Sona is an AI call-answering add-on for the Quo business phone system, formerly OpenPhone. Public material offers about 10 calls free, then $25 for 40 calls, $49 for 100, $99 for 250, or $199 for 600. Published overages range from $1.00 to $0.45 per call.

Sona advertises 24/7 answering, knowledge-based responses, lead and message capture, summaries, and integration with Quo call flows. Buyers need a paid Quo phone plan before adding Sona. If credits run out, Sona follows the configured fallback unless the customer enables overages.

## Fair comparison

Sona makes sense for teams that already want Quo's shared phone and messaging workspace. LobbyStack is independent receptionist software with public code, booking workflows, and a self-hosted path.

## Claims to avoid

Official product material emphasizes answering and message capture. Do not claim that Sona cannot book; say direct appointment execution is not advertised on the reviewed pages.
