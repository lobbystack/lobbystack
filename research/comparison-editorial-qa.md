## Mechanical checks

- No em dashes in the new article set.
- No filler openings from the stop-slop banned-phrase list.
- Each vendor receives a clear use case where it can be the better choice.
- Per-call, per-minute, per-caller, and per-interaction prices are not converted into one another without a call-pattern example.
- Hosted competitors are described as not advertising source access or self-hosting on the reviewed pages, rather than as incapable of offering them.
- Pricing and features carry the July 30, 2026 review date.

## Content-depth audit

The audit did not use a universal word-count target. Each page must answer the alternative or comparison intent with original evidence, a pricing model, a feature table, a buyer scenario, selection guidance, sources, and a next step.

- English articles contain 821 to 1,053 words.
- French articles contain 654 to 759 words.
- Nine leaner competitor pairs gained vendor-specific evaluation sections covering test calls, contract boundaries, billing units, handoff failures, or operating costs.
- The two topical comparisons already covered follow-up questions in more than 700 French words and more than 900 English words, so they did not receive filler.
