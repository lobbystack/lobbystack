# Smith.ai comparison research

Checked: 2026-07-30

## Official sources

- Product: https://smith.ai/ai-receptionist
- Pricing: https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist

## Publishable facts

Smith.ai combines an AI receptionist with access to a North American live-agent network. Public pricing lists a free plan for 25 calls, Pro from $150 for 75 calls, and Enterprise from $500 for 300 calls. The product advertises intake, qualification, scheduling, warm routing, English and Spanish service, recording, transcription, CRM connections, and Quality Studio testing.

## Fair comparison

Smith.ai has the strongest human-backup story in this competitor set. Buyers who want managed live-agent coverage may value that more than infrastructure ownership. LobbyStack offers lower-cost minute allowances, public code, and self-hosting. Custom AI prompting and unlimited custom workflows appear in Smith.ai's Enterprise offering.

## Claims to avoid

Do not describe Smith.ai as AI-only or compare call and minute allowances as if they were equivalent.
