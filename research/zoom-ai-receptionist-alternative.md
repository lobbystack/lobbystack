## Publishable facts

Zoom announced its standalone Virtual Agent Receptionist on July 9, 2026. Public pricing starts at $29.99 per month for 100 minutes or $24.99 with annual billing. Zoom says the product can work with an existing phone system.

Official pages advertise 24/7 answering, booking, contextual transfers, knowledge sources, Google, Microsoft, and Calendly integrations, SMS, more than 10 languages, transcripts, analytics, and Zoom's communications administration.

## Fair comparison

Zoom offers a low entry price and the operational credibility of a large communications vendor. LobbyStack's distinctions are public source code, self-hosting, and the option to control providers and infrastructure.

## Claims to avoid

The product is new. Do not infer maturity, uptime, or customer outcomes from Zoom's broader product history.
