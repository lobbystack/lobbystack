# Dialzara comparison research

Checked: 2026-07-30

## Official sources

- Pricing and feature matrix: https://dialzara.com/pricing

## Publishable facts

Dialzara sells inbound AI receptionist plans and optional outbound voice, SMS, chat, agency, and white-label products. Public inbound plans list $29 for 60 minutes, $99 for 220, $199 for 500, and $349 for 1,000. Published overages range from $0.48 to $0.35 per minute.

The service advertises recordings, summaries, notifications, knowledge uploads, transfers, calendar synchronization, multilingual calls, Make, Zapier, and API access on higher plans. Warm transfers, after-hours rules, bilingual support, and calendar sync begin on the $99 tier. SMS has a separate published monthly and usage cost.

## Fair comparison

Dialzara gives agencies and resellers several commercial paths. LobbyStack gives technical teams public code and self-hosted deployment. Buyers should compare the complete plan and add-on cost for the workflow they need.

## Claims to avoid

Do not mix inbound plan allowances with Dialzara's separate outbound or white-label offers.
