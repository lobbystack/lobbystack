# Research: how competitors run white-label and reseller programs

Research date: 2026-10-24

Scope: 30 AI receptionist products, voice AI platforms, and agency SaaS platforms. We read public pricing pages, partner pages, help centers, docs, terms, and the vendors' own videos. We did not sign up, log in, or book demos, so "not published" means the vendor's public pages don't say. Prices are as published on the research date.

Purpose: input for LobbyStack's white-label offer, where agencies resell LobbyStack under their own brand.

## Summary

- **The market has settled on one shape.** An agency pays a flat platform fee of $99 to $500 a month, plus wholesale usage at $0.05 to $0.20 a minute. It bills its clients through its own Stripe account, which it connects to the platform. Trillet, Autocalls, Famulor, Insighto, echowin, VoiceAI Connect, Stammer, Assistable and GoHighLevel all work this way, as do the Vapi and Retell wrappers.
- **Agencies charge their clients $150 to $300 a month for a receptionist.** Vendors' suggested prices cluster there: RingReady $150 to $300, Autocalls $199 to $299 for 300 to 500 minutes, Trillet and VoiceAI Connect about $250 to $300. LobbyStack sells Pro direct at $100 for 500 minutes, which sits below most of these retail prices and close to some wholesale ones.
- **The agency supports its own clients.** Every white-label program here puts front-line support on the agency. Vendasta sells setup and monthly support as a paid service, and Newo takes platform escalations. Others give the agency tools: Frontdesk's Intercom hookup, support links in the client portal, and branded help docs.
- **Branding has a baseline and a premium tier.** The baseline is logo, colors, company name, a custom subdomain via CNAME, and a branded login page. The premium tier, often a higher plan, adds a branded email sending domain, a root domain, a white-labeled API and docs domain, and a branded mobile app. Only GoHighLevel ($497 a month) and Vida offer the mobile app.
- **Client controls look alike everywhere.** You get one sub-account per client, a template to clone a working setup, toggles that hide features per client or per plan, a minute or spend cap per client, and an API to provision clients. Logging in as a client is less common. Famulor, Autocalls, VoiceAIWrapper, GoHighLevel and Vendasta document it, and Famulor writes each session to an audit log.
- **The infrastructure vendors don't sell a branded portal.** Retell says "the Retell dashboard itself is not rebranded" and sends agencies to wrapper products. Vapi's docs list four wrappers. That gap is why Vapify, VoiceAIWrapper, ChatDash and Voicerr exist.
- **Two vendors pulled back in 2026.** Synthflow removed in-product Stripe rebilling on 15 September 2026, and its white label now starts at $30,000 a year under contract. Frontdesk replaced its self-serve $54.99-per-receptionist reseller plan with an application and custom plans that start at $500 a month.
- **Referral programs pay 10% to 30% recurring.** Frontdesk pays 30%, Upfirst 25%, Rosie 20%, and Smith.ai 10% to 15%. Goodcall, Rosie, Upfirst, Phonely and Smith.ai offer no white label at all.

## Three program models

| Model | What the agency gets | Who bills the end client | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| True white label | The agency's brand on the portal, login, emails and domain; one sub-account per client | The agency, usually through its own Stripe connected to the platform | Frontdesk, Trillet, echowin (White Label tier), VoiceAI Connect, RingReady, Vida, Autocalls, Famulor, Insighto, Stammer, Assistable, GoHighLevel, Vendasta (Professional and above), Vapify, VoiceAIWrapper |
| Agency-managed vendor accounts | Access to several client accounts under the vendor's brand | The vendor or the agency, with no rebilling tools | Goodcall, Rosie, Retell (a workspace per client), Vapi (organizations), Thoughtly (workspaces), echowin Grey Label, Vendasta Starter |
| Referral or revenue share | A commission. The vendor owns the client. | The vendor | Smith.ai, Upfirst, Bland, Thoughtly, Newo (partner tiers), Vida (affiliate option), Frontdesk (affiliate) |

Several vendors run two or more of these at once. Frontdesk, Newo, Trillet and Vida each pair a white-label tier with a referral program.

Most programs take a fee plus usage. Three pricing designs stand out:

1. **Fee plus included minutes plus overage**: Trillet, Autocalls, Insighto. This one is the easiest for an agency to model.
2. **Per-client fee that falls as the agency fee rises**: VoiceAI Connect ($29.99 a client on Free, $9.99 on Pro, $0 on Scale). It lets an agency start for $0 and grow.
3. **Per-client wholesale at a discount to direct prices, unlimited minutes**: RingReady. The agency's margin is the gap between its price and RingReady's, and RingReady publishes both.

### Where LobbyStack sits

## How the programs work

### Billing

The platform charges the agency, and the agency charges its clients. Almost every program splits it that way.

- **Platform to agency.** Most programs use a prepaid wallet with auto-recharge: Trillet ($5 minimum top-up, recharge at $10 or more), echowin, Frontdesk's credits (one credit is about one minute at 12 cents), GoHighLevel, Famulor and Assistable. The rest add usage to a monthly subscription. When the agency wallet runs dry, calls stop for every client: echowin, Assistable and GoHighLevel each document this.
- **Agency to client.** The agency connects Stripe, most often through Stripe Connect OAuth (VoiceAI Connect, Vapify, Stammer, Famulor, Autocalls, Assistable, GoHighLevel). Insighto and VoiceAIWrapper ask for a pasted Stripe key instead, and Vida takes Stripe or Chargebee. echowin, RingReady and now Synthflow leave client billing to the agency's own tools.
- **Plan builder.** The agency defines plans with a name, monthly and yearly price, included minutes, overage rate, trial length, feature toggles and limits. Clients subscribe on the agency's domain. GoHighLevel's SaaS Configurator sets the bar here. Its checkout link creates the sub-account and loads a snapshot. Autocalls and Famulor block a plan priced below platform cost, and Famulor adds a 10% payment-fee reserve to that floor.
- **Client wallet with markup.** The client prepays into a wallet that the platform debits at the agency's price. Agencies set the price as a multiplier (GoHighLevel's per-service sliders, Insighto's four cost multipliers) or a fixed rate per minute. Vapify holds any call that bills below cost for review.
- **Usage reporting.** GoHighLevel shows revenue, cost, profit and margin per client. Famulor shows MRR, run rate and margin per plan, and Autocalls MRR and ARPU.

Running rebilling means handling client tax, refunds and failed payments. Autocalls blocks client upgrades until the agency sets up Stripe Tax on its Connect account, and GoHighLevel emails the remaining wallet balance to the agency to refund when SaaS mode turns off. Synthflow's docs give no reason for dropping rebilling. The migration guide tells agencies to read `minutes_used` from the API and bill from their own systems.

### Client accounts and controls

- **Sub-account per client.** Each client gets its own workspace with its own agents, numbers and call history. Frontdesk also lets a client register through a form on the agency's site.
- **Templates.** GoHighLevel snapshots, Vendasta blueprints, Synthflow Products, Assistable account defaults, Frontdesk's "Copy existing receptionist", and Autocalls prompt templates all clone a setup. Snapshots can carry Voice AI agents, but phone numbers never copy over. GoHighLevel can push a refreshed snapshot to linked accounts.
- **Feature visibility.** The agency hides pages or makes them read-only per client or per plan. Frontdesk splits this into Visibility Control and Gating Control. Synthflow has toggles per module and per agent editor tab. Autocalls hides pages and features one by one, Assistable has 18 navigation switches, and Trillet offers a "Simplified" experience that hides workflows, roles and API keys.
- **Caps.** Frontdesk caps minutes per client. echowin sets a monthly credit limit per client. GoHighLevel sets an AI spend limit up to $1,000 a month, alerts at 70% to 100%, and lets the agency choose between notifying and blocking at the limit.
- **Logging in as the client.** Famulor's "User view" shows a banner and audits each session. Autocalls has "Allow impersonate owner", VoiceAIWrapper "Login as User", and GoHighLevel "Switch to Sub-Account". echowin's Super Admin access expires after a set time. Insighto has no such feature, and Trillet requires the agency to join the client workspace.
- **Live monitoring.** Famulor lets the agency listen, whisper, take over or hang up on any client's live call.
- **API provisioning.** Autocalls, Famulor, Insighto, Synthflow, Vida, Assistable and GoHighLevel document endpoints to create clients. Assistable's create call is idempotent per GoHighLevel location. Retell has no API for workspaces.

### Support

In every white-label program, the agency supports its clients. Newo spells out the tiers: "You handle front line, Newo handles platform escalations." Vendasta sells setup and $49-a-month support services that agencies can resell, plus grey-labeled guides they can send to clients.

## What the dashboards look like

### Agency dashboard

The complete programs share these sections:

1. **Clients.** A table with each client's plan, status, balance and usage, with buttons to add, copy or open a client.
2. **Plans or pricing.** A plan builder with included minutes, overage, trial, and features. GoHighLevel's form includes Plan Details, Category, Pricing, Features & Snapshot, Add-ons, Trial & Credits, and Usage Billing.
3. **Branding.** Name, logo, favicon, colors and login page fields.
4. **Domains and email.** Domain verification status, the DNS records to add, and the sending domain's DKIM records.
5. **Billing.** The agency's own wallet, auto-recharge and invoices, plus the Stripe connection.
6. **Analytics.** Usage per client, and on the stronger products revenue: Famulor shows Estimated MRR, Revenue per subscriber, Subscription margin per plan and Low-credit customers. GoHighLevel shows Total Revenue, Total Cost and Net Profit per client, and Autocalls MRR and ARPU.
7. **Templates, team, and API keys.**

echowin's partner portal mockup shows Active clients, Partner MRR and Calls handled. VoiceAI Connect's agency dashboard shows MRR, churn, client count and call volume.

### Client portal

The client portal is the vendor's normal product under the agency's brand, minus whatever the agency hides. Clients see call logs with recordings, transcripts and summaries; usage charts; their agent settings if allowed; and a billing page to subscribe, top up, and download invoices. Locked features show an upgrade prompt at the agency's price (Vendasta's "Upgrade to gain access" chip, Frontdesk's Gating Control). GoHighLevel shows unpaid clients a lockout screen, and Vapify can require an active subscription before a client gets in.

## What LobbyStack has today

LobbyStack already has some of the pieces:

- **Deployment branding.** A self-hosted deployment can set its own app name, logo, website, terms, privacy, docs and contact links, and the SMS HELP replies use the name. The setting covers the whole deployment, not one partner, and the cloud refuses it: `DEPLOYMENT_MODE=cloud` hides the onboarding step and `POST /api/branding/initial` returns 403 ([branding doc](../docs/branding.md), [`deployment_branding` table](../packages/db/src/schema/index.ts#L1430-L1441)).
- **Partner platform keys.** Each `PLATFORM_API_KEYS` entry names a partner and can carry the platform-only `businesses:create` and `users:create` scopes. `platform_partner_businesses` links each business a partner's key created to that partner. Operators set keys in an environment variable, so partners can't create their own ([`platformKeys.ts`](../packages/domain/src/server/publicApi/platformKeys.ts#L12-L36), [schema](../packages/db/src/schema/index.ts#L1443-L1458), [scopes](../packages/shared/src/publicApi/constants.ts#L37)).
- **Multiple businesses per account.** Accounts can hold several businesses with memberships, roles and workspace switching. One account can own up to 10 businesses ([`tenancy.ts`](../packages/domain/src/server/tenancy.ts#L34)).
- **Affiliate program.** LobbyStack tracks referral codes, attributions and stats ([schema](../packages/db/src/schema/index.ts#L1120-L1161)).
- **Billing.** Polar bills each business for Starter or Pro, plus metered voice minutes, alert SMS and outbound attempts ([Polar doc](../docs/providers/polar.md)).
- **MIT license.** Anyone can fork and rebrand the code. The license grants no rights to the LobbyStack name ([MIT research](lobbystack-mit-license-ai-receptionist-resellers.md)).

## SMS sender rules

Checked 2026-10-24. LobbyStack's cloud texts for every business from one toll-free number that Twilio verified under LobbyStack's brand.

- Twilio says a toll-free number used for messaging "must map 1:1 to a single business" ([error 30478](https://www.twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30478)). One number can serve many businesses only when the platform runs the whole opt-in and writes every message ([Twilio ISV guide](https://help.twilio.com/articles/13263383206299-Toll-Free-Verification-for-ISVs), [error 30479](https://www.twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30479)).
- Twilio's checklist asks for the business name in every message. It also asks for the verified brand in the opt-in confirmation and in HELP and STOP replies ([Twilio onboarding guide](https://help.twilio.com/articles/13264118705051)).
- CTIA requires an opt-in confirmation before any other text in a recurring program. It must give the program name, help, opt-out, frequency, and fees ([CTIA Messaging Principles, §5.1.2.1](https://api.ctia.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/230523-CTIA-Messaging-Principles-and-Best-Practices-FINAL.pdf)). CTIA's short code handbook, which Twilio cites for toll-free, asks for the brand name in HELP and STOP replies too ([Short Code Handbook v1.9, §3.4](https://api.ctia.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/CTIA-Short-Code-Monitoring-Handbook-v1.9-FINAL.pdf)).
- Fresha follows this pattern. Its first text names Fresha as the booking platform for the business, and the next text names only the business. Its terms have customers consent to texts from Fresha ([Fresha terms](https://terms.fresha.com/terms-service)). Its pages don't say whether the first text goes out once per phone number or every time.
- Rewording HELP or START counts as a content change and needs no new verification. Changing who the sender is does, and a number can't send while its verification is pending ([Twilio console guide](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/toll-free/console-onboarding), [Twilio help](https://help.twilio.com/articles/5377174717595)).
- A sender per business would let every text name only the business. The options are a toll-free number verified to the business ($2.15 a month, 3 to 5 business days) or a 10DLC brand and campaign ($19.50 once, $1.50 to $10 a month, 10 to 15 days for the campaign) ([Twilio SMS pricing](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/sms/pricing/us), [10DLC fees](https://help.twilio.com/articles/1260803965530-What-pricing-and-fees-are-associated-with-the-A2P-10DLC-service-), [10DLC quickstart](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc/quickstart)). We chose to keep the one verified number.
- Today's texts have gaps for direct businesses too. The verbal consent script names no business ([`capabilities.ts`](../packages/agent-core/src/capabilities.ts#L138-L142)), no opt-in confirmation goes out, and appointment-change codes say "LobbyStack verification code" ([`verificationCode.ts`](../packages/domain/src/server/verificationCode.ts#L23)).

#### Twilio and Stripe Connect: how these platforms use them

- **Stripe, HighLevel:** the agency links its own Stripe account under Agency View > Settings > Stripe ("Connect with Stripe") for SaaS V1 ([SaaS Configurator guide](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000008015-getting-started-with-the-saas-configurator)). The agency's Stripe makes every charge to a client, and the agency pays Stripe's processing fees ([wallets article](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000002095-rebilling-reselling-and-wallets-explained)).
- **Stripe, Vendasta:** a partner can connect its own Stripe account, and Vendasta adds a 0.75% fee, or it can use a platform-created account ([connect payments](https://docs.vendasta.com/learn/getting-started/connect-payments-and-billing/)). This matches Stripe Connect's direct-charge model, where the connected account sells to its own customers and the platform takes an `application_fee_amount` (my inference) ([Stripe charge types](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/charges), [Stripe direct charges](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges)).
- **Stripe, Assistable and Trillet:** Assistable uses Stripe OAuth and keeps client customer records in the agency's Stripe ([connect Stripe](https://docs.assistable.ai/v3/guides/billing/connect-stripe.md)). Trillet bills clients through the agency's own Stripe with one-time or recurring invoices ([Trillet knowledge base](https://www.trillet.ai/llms.txt)).
- **Pattern:** HighLevel and Assistable record usage against a prepaid client wallet and refill it with card charges ([wallets article](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000002095-rebilling-reselling-and-wallets-explained), [billing guide](https://docs.assistable.ai/v3/billing-guide.md)). None of the docs I read describe Stripe metered billing for usage.
- **Twilio:** subaccounts let a platform keep each customer's usage separate. All usage bills to the main account, which allows 1,000 subaccounts by default ([Twilio subaccounts](https://www.twilio.com/docs/iam/api/subaccounts)). HighLevel's agency-managed Twilio option maps each location to its own Twilio sub-account. LC Phone, HighLevel's native phone system, supports rebilling without an agency Twilio account ([agency-managed Twilio](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001179177-what-is-agency-managed-twilio-and-phone-rebilling-in-highlevel-)), and HighLevel prices it to match Twilio ([pricing guide](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001156-highlevel-pricing-guide)).
- **Bring your own carrier:** Assistable and Trillet let agencies bring Twilio, Telnyx, or SIP numbers and charge no telephony fee on them ([rate sheet](https://www.assistable.ai/pricing), [Trillet white-label pricing](https://www.trillet.ai/whitelabel/pricing)). Vendasta assigns the number itself ([AI Voice Receptionist](https://docs.vendasta.com/ai/ai-workforce/ai-voice-receptionist/)), and its carrier is not published.
