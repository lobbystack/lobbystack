## Publishable facts

ElevenLabs launched Reception, branded "Reception by ElevenAgents" at reception.ai, on September 16, 2026. It targets home services, professional services, real estate and property management, and salons.

Plans use credits. A phone minute costs 1 credit, a web chat minute 0.5 credits, and an assistant message 0.1 credits.

- Basic: $29 per month or $288 per year ($24 per month). 75 credits, 1 receptionist, 1 phone number, 1 location, 5 knowledge sources, 1 concurrent call, $0.45 per extra credit.
- Plus: $79 per month or $792 per year ($66 per month). 275 credits, 3 phone numbers, 10 knowledge sources, 3 concurrent calls, $0.38 per extra credit.
- Premium: $199 per month or $1,992 per year ($166 per month). 1,000 credits, 3 receptionists, 5 phone numbers, 20 locations, 20 knowledge sources, 10 concurrent calls, $0.30 per extra credit.
- Trial: 14 days, 30 credits, no credit card.

Every plan includes Google Calendar, Zapier, webhooks and MCP. Reception answers in 70+ languages with automatic detection, books and reschedules appointments, transfers calls by rule, takes messages with priority levels, offers a public booking page, web chat, a CRM, analytics, summaries, transcripts and recordings. A staff-first mode lets employees answer during chosen hours before Reception picks up. Its FAQ says it does not support HIPAA compliance and sends healthcare customers to ElevenAgents enterprise.

## Fair comparison

Reception's strengths are voice quality, language coverage, the staff-first mode and integrations on every plan. LobbyStack includes twice the phone minutes at the same $24 annual entry price, charges less than half the overage rate, keeps a free plan with no expiry, and publishes its code under MIT for self-hosting and resale.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not say Reception lacks SMS or texting. Its public pages do not mention it either way.
- Do not use "from $22 per month". Third-party pages show it, but the official pricing docs list $24 per month billed annually.
- Do not give LobbyStack a language count. LobbyStack answers in the caller's language and switches automatically, but it publishes no count.
- LobbyStack has no per-plan limit on concurrent calls in code, so "not capped by plan" is accurate. Do not promise unlimited calls.
- Do not say short or spam calls are free on LobbyStack. The code bills every call's full duration.
