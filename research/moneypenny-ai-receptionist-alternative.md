# Moneypenny AI Receptionist comparison research

Checked: 2026-07-30

## Official sources

- Product: https://moneypenny.com/us/ai-receptionist/
- Pricing: https://moneypenny.com/us/plans-pricing-ai/

## Publishable facts

Moneypenny combines AI answering with its established live-receptionist operation. Public plans list $69 for 25 calls, $99 for 50, $199 for 100, $435 for 250, and $775 for 500. Published overages range from $2.49 to $1.69 per call.

The product advertises qualification, scheduling, routing, FAQs, data collection, voice customization, analytics, transcripts, SMS follow-up, managed onboarding, and escalation to the buyer's team or Moneypenny staff.

## Fair comparison

Moneypenny has a strong managed-service and human-escalation offer. LobbyStack has lower-cost usage tiers, public code, and self-hosting. Buyers should decide whether managed human backup or infrastructure control carries more value for their calls.

## Claims to avoid

Do not compare Moneypenny's per-call plans with LobbyStack's minutes without an example call mix.
