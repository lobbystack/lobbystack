## Publishable facts

Upfirst sells a self-serve AI answering service for small businesses. Its public plans list $24.95 for 30 calls, $59.95 for 90, $159.95 for 300, and $299 for 600. Public material advertises recordings, transcripts, summaries, warm and waterfall transfers, calendar booking, in-call texting, spam filtering, caller memory, more than 35 languages, Zapier, and CRM connections.

## Fair comparison

Upfirst offers a polished setup and broad receptionist features. Its per-call billing can suit long conversations. A business with many short calls should compare the call allowance against LobbyStack's minute allowance. LobbyStack's clearest distinctions are public source code, self-hosting, and control over the deployment and providers.

## Claims to avoid

Do not claim that Upfirst lacks a feature unless its current documentation rules it out. Say that the reviewed pages do not advertise self-hosting or public source code.
