# LobbyStack

LobbyStack answers businesses' phone calls and website chats with an AI receptionist. Some businesses sign up directly, and others come through a partner.

## Language

**Business**:
An organization whose calls and website chats LobbyStack answers. It signs up directly or through a partner.
_Avoid_: Client, tenant, customer

**Partner**:
A company that sets up and runs businesses on LobbyStack and owns its relationship with them. It works through the API, or under its own brand as a white-label partner.
_Avoid_: Reseller, agency

**White-label partner**:
A partner whose businesses see the partner's brand, domain, and bills instead of LobbyStack's.
_Avoid_: Agency, white-label agency

**Affiliate**:
A person who refers businesses to LobbyStack for a commission. An affiliate never manages the businesses it refers.
_Avoid_: Partner, referrer
