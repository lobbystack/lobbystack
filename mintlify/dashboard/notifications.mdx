The bell beside the workspace name, at the top of the sidebar, lists what needs your attention in the active workspace. A dot on the bell means you have something unread. On a phone, open **Menu**: the bell sits beside the workspace name, and the **Menu** tab shows a dot while anything is unread.

Calls, texts, chats and bookings don't appear here. Find them in the [Inbox](/dashboard/inbox) and on [Home](/dashboard/home).

## What the bell lists

Items that need action come first:

- Your free trial ends in 4 days or tomorrow, or it ended
- Your trial minutes are used up, or your workspace used 80% or all of its minutes this month
- A payment failed, or your plan ends at the end of the period
- A phone number purchase failed, a number is about to be released, or a number was released
- A calendar connection stopped syncing
- Instagram needs reconnecting
- LobbyStack turned off a webhook endpoint because every delivery failed for a day
- A website import failed

Updates follow, newest first: a website import finished, a teammate accepted your invite, or LobbyStack added a feature.

Each person sees the items their role lets them act on. Owners and admins see billing and setup items. Everyone sees product updates.

## Open an item

1. Click the bell.
2. Click an item. LobbyStack marks it read and opens the page where you fix or check it, such as **Business** > **Knowledge** for a failed website import.

An item that needs action stays in the list after you read it, until the problem is fixed. A failed payment, for example, stays until your card works again.

## Mark everything read

Click **Mark all as read** at the top of the list. The dot goes away for you; your teammates keep their own unread items.
