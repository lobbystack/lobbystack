The **Appointments** page lists upcoming bookings for the active workspace. Use it to review the schedule and check whether each booking reached the connected calendar.

## Review the schedule

Each row shows:

- Appointment time
- Customer name
- Service
- Assigned staff member
- Appointment status
- Calendar synchronization status

LobbyStack displays each appointment in its saved timezone.

## Book an appointment

1. Click **New appointment**.
2. Choose a contact, or click **New contact** and enter a name and phone number. The list shows contacts that have a phone number.
3. Choose a service. When more than one employee is assigned to it, you can pick one or keep **Anyone available**.
4. Pick a day and an open time, then click **Book appointment**.

LobbyStack books it the way the agent does: it checks availability, follows the same rules for confirmation and reminder texts, and adds the appointment to the connected calendar. Viewers can't book appointments. If someone takes the time before you book, pick another one from the refreshed list.

## Refresh appointment data

Click **Refresh** to request the latest appointment list. The page also updates when LobbyStack receives an appointment event.

## Investigate a calendar issue

If the **Calendar** column shows a synchronization issue, check the Google Calendar, Cal.com or Calendly connection under **Business** > **Integrations**. Correct any account or permission problem before relying on live availability.

Connect [Google Calendar](/integrations/google-calendar), [Cal.com](/integrations/calcom) or [Calendly](/integrations/calendly) before allowing the agent to offer times. Configure caller cancellation and rescheduling under [Appointment changes](/agent/appointment-changes).
