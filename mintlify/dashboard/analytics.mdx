The **Analytics** page helps you understand how customers are using your receptionist. It summarizes weekly activity and shows whether calls, messages, appointments, and average call duration are moving up or down.

![LobbyStack Analytics page](/images/screenshots/analytics.png)

_Analytics helps you spot workload changes and decide where to tune your agent or follow-up process._

## What analytics shows

| Section | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Booked value**, **Time saved**, **Calls without transfer** | What your receptionist booked, the talk time it took off your team, and how many calls it finished without a transfer. |
| **Engagement overview** | Weekly calls and messages in one chart. |
| **Calls this week** | Call count and change compared with the previous week. |
| **Messages this week** | SMS conversation activity and weekly change. |
| **Appointments booked** | Booking volume and weekly change. |
| **Average call duration** | Typical call length and week-over-week change. |
| **Call outcomes** | Completed, transferred, live, and missed call outcomes. |
| **Channels** | The share of activity coming from voice, SMS, and other channels. |

## See what your receptionist booked and saved

The three figures at the top of the page cover the dates you pick, and each one is compared with the period of the same length just before.

| Figure | How LobbyStack counts it |
| --- | --- |
| **Booked value** | The price of each appointment your receptionist booked by call, text, or chat in those dates, after any service discount. A fixed price counts in full, a starting price at its minimum, and a price range at its low end. |
| **Time saved** | The talk time on calls your receptionist handled, shown in hours and minutes. |
| **Calls without transfer** | The share of those calls that ended without your receptionist transferring the caller to your team. |

Booked value leaves out:

- Cancelled appointments
- Appointments your team booked in the dashboard or through the API
- Services priced in person or without a price

LobbyStack uses each service's current price, so changing a price also changes the estimate for earlier dates. The figure appears in the currency set for your business.

Time saved and the transfer share leave out spam calls and calls shorter than 10 seconds, the same calls your plan doesn't bill. Website chats don't add to time saved, since they have no talk time.

## Review performance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Analytics">
    In the dashboard sidebar, go to **Manage** > **Analytics**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check weekly movement">
    Look at the metric cards to see whether call, message, appointment, and duration trends changed from the previous week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review outcomes">
    Use **Call outcomes** to see whether calls are completing, transferring, or being missed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare channels">
    Use **Channels** to understand how much activity is coming through voice, SMS, and other sources.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to read the trends

| Signal | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Calls are rising** | Review whether the team needs more coverage for follow-ups, transfers, or booking requests. |
| **Messages are rising** | Check active conversations and decide whether more threads should stay automated or be handled by staff. |
| **Appointments are rising** | Review upcoming appointments and make sure services and calendar settings still match your capacity. |
| **Missed or failed outcomes increase** | Open recent calls, review transcripts, and adjust knowledge, rules, or transfer settings if callers are getting stuck. |
| **Average duration changes sharply** | Longer calls can mean callers need more explanation; shorter calls can mean callers found answers sooner or ended calls early. |

## Weekly review routine

1. Start with the metric cards to see what changed.
2. Review **Call outcomes** for missed, failed, or transfer-heavy patterns.
3. Open **Calls** for examples behind the trend.
4. Update **Knowledge** or **Rules** when callers are repeatedly asking about the same topic.
5. Recheck Analytics after the next busy period to see whether the change helped.

## When to use it

Use Analytics when you want to understand workload, spot changes in caller behavior, or decide whether your agent setup needs adjustment. For individual conversations, use **Calls**, **Messages**, and **Contacts**.
