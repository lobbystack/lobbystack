The **Contacts** page lists the people connected to your calls, messages, and appointments. Contacts are created automatically from phone interactions and are updated as new activity happens.

## Contact list

Each row can show:

- Contact name or unknown contact.
- Phone number and email if available.
- Activity counts for calls and messages.
- Appointment count.
- Last interaction time.
- Blocked status.

Use search to filter by name, phone number, or email.

## Contact detail

Open a contact to view:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Activity** | Calls, SMS messages, and appointment activity in chronological order. |
| **Appointments** | Service, date and time, status, channel, and calendar sync state. |
| **Details** | Contact info, blocking state, SMS consent, keypad PIN, and system metadata. |

## Blocking contacts

Blocking a contact stops new SMS replies and causes new inbound calls from that contact to hang up immediately. You can unblock the contact later from the same menu.

## SMS consent

The contact detail page shows whether the contact is subscribed or opted out of SMS, when the consent state changed, and the source of that change when available.

## Keypad PIN

When AI settings require a keypad PIN to change appointments, callers type the PIN saved on their contact on the phone keypad. On the **Details** tab, admins and owners can set a PIN, set a new one, or remove it. The tab shows whether the contact has a PIN, when it was set, and whether it's locked after too many wrong tries. LobbyStack can't show a saved PIN, so give the customer the PIN when you set it. See [Configure appointment changes](/agent/ai-settings/appointment-changes#set-up-keypad-pins).

## Deleting contacts

Contacts with linked conversations or appointments stay in place so activity history remains complete. Contacts without linked records can be deleted from the contact detail page.

<Note>
  Contact deletion is permanent. Linked conversations and appointments stay protected so your activity history remains intact.
</Note>
