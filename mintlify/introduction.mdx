LobbyStack is an AI agent for businesses that rely on phone calls. It answers calls through a dedicated business phone number, uses your saved business knowledge to respond to callers, helps with appointment booking and appointment changes when your services and calendar are connected, and keeps calls, messages, contacts, appointments, transcripts, and recordings organized in one dashboard.

Start with the setup guide if you are setting up LobbyStack for the first time. If your workspace is already live, use the dashboard guides to review activity, tune your agent, and keep follow-ups moving.

<Tip>
  Start with a free account. During setup, you pick a phone number, then test your agent in your browser. Without a paid plan, that number is free for 14 days. [Check where numbers are available](/getting-started/number-countries).
</Tip>

## What LobbyStack helps with

- Answer inbound calls through a dedicated LobbyStack number.
- Open calls in one of eight languages, then switch to the caller's language when they speak another one.
- Search knowledge from text entries, uploaded documents, and imported public website pages.
- Help callers schedule, cancel, or reschedule appointments when services and Google Calendar are configured.
- Transfer a live call to your transfer number, or to the employee a caller asks for, when transfer policy allows it.
- Take callback or follow-up messages when a transfer is not possible or a caller wants staff to follow up.
- Store calls, transcripts, recordings, contacts, SMS conversations, appointments, and dashboard summaries.
- Support hosted plans, usage tracking, and self-hosted deployments.

## Main dashboard areas

The sidebar's main menu lists **Home**, **Inbox**, **Appointments**, **Contacts**, **Analytics**, **Agents**, **Business** and **Settings**. **Agents**, **Business** and **Settings** open their own menus in the sidebar. The row at the top of each, with a back arrow, brings back the main menu. Press `Cmd+K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows) or click **Search** at the top of the sidebar to find any page, setting, agent, contact or conversation.

| Area | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home**, **Inbox**, **Appointments**, **Contacts** | Review daily activity, calls and conversations, caller profiles, and bookings. |
| **Analytics** | Track calls, chats and bookings over time, by agent when you have several. |
| **Agents** | Configure how each AI agent greets callers, behaves, transfers, books, and which channels it answers. |
| **Business** | Manage the business details, knowledge, services, employees and calendar connections every agent shares. |
| **Settings** | Manage team, usage, billing, API keys, webhooks and your own account. |
