Connect Cal.com when your team already takes bookings there. Your AI receptionist books each appointment as a Cal.com booking of one event type, and it treats every booking on the account as busy time, so it doesn't offer times already booked through your Cal.com booking page.

<Note>
  Add the services callers can request before you connect Cal.com. LobbyStack compares their lengths with the event type you pick.
</Note>

## What Cal.com receives

For each appointment, Cal.com receives:

- **Attendee name and phone number**: the caller's, so you see who booked
- **Attendee email**: a stand-in address unique to the appointment, so Cal.com doesn't email the caller
- **Booking notes**: the caller's name, phone number and email, for your team

The AI receptionist sends the caller's confirmation and reminders itself. Cal.com still sends your team its host notifications.

## Connect Cal.com

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    In the dashboard, open **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the Cal.com connection">
    Find **Cal.com** and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize LobbyStack">
    Sign in to Cal.com and approve the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an event type">
    Back in the dashboard, choose an event type under **Event type**. LobbyStack saves your choice right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

LobbyStack starts reading your Cal.com bookings, and the calendars Cal.com checks for conflicts, within a few seconds.

## Set up the event type in Cal.com

After you pick an event type, the dialog lists anything to fix in Cal.com:

- **Durations**: the event type must offer every length your services last. Add the missing lengths under the event type's duration settings, or Cal.com books its default length.
- **SMS workflow**: turn off any SMS workflow on the event type. Otherwise callers get texts from Cal.com and from your AI receptionist.
- **Schedule**: the event type's availability must cover your opening hours. Cal.com turns down bookings outside its schedule, and the dashboard alerts you when a booking fails to sync.

## Change the event type

1. Open **Integrations**.
2. Click the Cal.com card.
3. Choose another event type.

New appointments use the new event type. Upcoming appointments move to it at the next sync.

## Reconnect or disconnect

If Cal.com stops accepting the connection, the card shows that reconnect is required. Open the Cal.com card and click **Reconnect**.

To remove the integration, open the Cal.com card and click **Disconnect**. Bookings already in Cal.com stay there.
