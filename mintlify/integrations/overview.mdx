LobbyStack connects to Google Calendar, Cal.com, Calendly and Instagram, and sends events to your own tools with webhooks. Calendars connect in **Business** > **Connections**, and every agent books on them. Instagram connects on an agent's **Channels**. Webhooks have their own page, **Settings** > **Webhooks**. To connect a CRM or Zapier, use webhooks and the [LobbyStack API](/api/overview).

Each integration guide explains what the connection does, what access it needs, and how to confirm it is working.

## Google Calendar

Connect Google Calendar when you want LobbyStack to help callers book appointments. After you authorize Google, choose the calendar that should receive bookings.

## Cal.com

Connect Cal.com when your team already takes bookings there. After you authorize Cal.com, choose the event type your AI agent books.

## Calendly

Connect Calendly when your team already takes bookings there on a paid plan. After you authorize Calendly, choose the event type your AI agent books.

## Instagram

Connect Instagram when people message your business there. Your agent answers with the knowledge it uses on calls, and you can take a conversation over from the [Inbox](/dashboard/inbox). Connect it from the **Channels** page of the agent that should answer.

## Webhooks

Add a webhook when another tool should react to LobbyStack, for example to log each finished call in your CRM.

## Where to find each one

- **Google Calendar**, **Cal.com** and **Calendly**: **Business** > **Connections**
- **Instagram**: the agent's **Channels**
- **Webhooks**: **Settings** > **Webhooks**
