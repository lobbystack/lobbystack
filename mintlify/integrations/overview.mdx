The **Integrations** page connects LobbyStack to Google Calendar and Instagram, and sends events to your own tools with webhooks. To connect a CRM or Zapier, use webhooks and the [LobbyStack API](/api/overview).

Each integration guide explains what the connection does, what access it needs, and how to confirm it is working.

## Google Calendar

Connect Google Calendar when you want LobbyStack to help callers book appointments. After you authorize Google, choose the calendar that should receive bookings.

## Instagram

Connect Instagram when people message your business there. Your receptionist answers with the knowledge it uses on calls, and you can take a conversation over from **Messages**.

## Webhooks

Add a webhook when another tool should react to LobbyStack, for example to log each finished call in your CRM.

## Where to find it

Open **Manage** > **Integrations** from the dashboard sidebar.
