This tutorial follows the onboarding flow in the LobbyStack dashboard from account creation to your first test call.

<Note>
  No credit card is required to create an account. Free plans can try LobbyStack with dashboard test calls. Dedicated business numbers are included on Starter and Pro. Hosted usage limits are shown in **Settings** after you sign in.
</Note>

## Before you begin

You need:

- An email address and password.
- A mobile phone number that can receive an SMS verification code.
- A public website URL if you want LobbyStack to import website knowledge during onboarding.

## Onboarding steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to the LobbyStack sign-up page, enter your email address and password, and submit the form.

    LobbyStack opens onboarding after signup.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name your business">
    Enter the business name that LobbyStack should show across the dashboard and customer-facing details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import your website">
    Enter a public website URL to start a background website import, or choose **Skip for now**.

    Website import crawls public pages such as services, about, FAQ, hours, and contact pages. Private pages, local network URLs, and login-only pages are not imported.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add knowledge documents">
    Upload supported business documents, add text, or skip the step. You can manage these sources later under **Agent** > **Knowledge**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the greeting">
    Write the opening line callers should hear. You can update voice and transfer behavior later under **Agent** > **AI settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your mobile number">
    Enter a mobile number in international format and request a verification code. Enter the six-digit code you receive by SMS.

    LobbyStack uses the verified number for account security, alerts, and local-number suggestions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select Free, Starter, or Pro. Free includes dashboard test calls without a dedicated public number. Starter and Pro include one dedicated business number.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Claim a phone number on a paid plan">
    On Starter or Pro, review the suggested business number or click **Pick a different number** to search by city, area code, or toll-free number.

    Click **Continue** to claim the selected number. Calls to that number route to your AI receptionist. Free plans skip this step and can upgrade later from **Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish onboarding">
    Select how you heard about LobbyStack, then open the dashboard. Review **AI settings**, **Knowledge**, **Services**, and **Rules** before sending live calls to the number.
  </Step>
</Steps>
