This tutorial follows the onboarding flow in the LobbyStack dashboard, from account creation to the first call to your new number.

<Note>
  You don't need a credit card. Without a paid plan, your phone number is free for 14 days. Starter and Pro include one number. After you sign in, **Settings** shows your hosted usage limits. [Check where numbers are available](/getting-started/number-countries).
</Note>

## Before you begin

You need:

- An email address and password.
- A mobile phone that can receive a text message. LobbyStack can't verify internet (VoIP) numbers.
- A public website URL if you want LobbyStack to import website knowledge during onboarding.

## Onboarding steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to the LobbyStack sign-up page, enter your email address and password, and submit the form.

    LobbyStack opens onboarding after signup.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name your business">
    Enter the business name that LobbyStack should show across the dashboard and customer-facing details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import your website">
    Enter a public website URL to start a background website import, or choose **I don't have a website**.

    Website import crawls public pages such as services, about, FAQ, hours, and contact pages. Private pages, local network URLs, and login-only pages are not imported.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add knowledge documents">
    Upload supported business documents, add text, or skip the step. You can manage these sources later under **Agent** > **Knowledge**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the greeting">
    Write the opening line callers should hear. You can update voice and transfer behavior later under **Agent** > **AI settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hear your receptionist">
    Start a test call in your browser and talk to your receptionist the way a caller would. Allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your mobile number">
    Pick your phone's country, enter your mobile number, and enter the 6-digit code LobbyStack texts you. Your mobile number has to be from the country you pick, and that country decides your LobbyStack number's country.

    During a free trial, your receptionist transfers calls only to this mobile number.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your number">
    Pick a number from the list. Without a paid plan, it's free for 14 days. See [how the free trial works](/getting-started/claim-your-number#free-trial-number).

    If LobbyStack doesn't sell numbers in your country yet, ask for an email when it does, and keep testing your receptionist in your browser.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Call your number">
    Call your new number from any phone. Your receptionist answers with your greeting.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Review **AI settings**, **Knowledge**, **Services**, and **Rules** before you share the number with callers.
  </Step>
</Steps>
