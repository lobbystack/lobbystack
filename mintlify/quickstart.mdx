This tutorial follows the onboarding flow in the LobbyStack dashboard, from account creation to your first test of your agent.

<Note>
  You don't need a credit card. Without a paid plan, your phone number is free for 14 days. Starter and Pro include one number. After you sign in, **Settings** > **Usage** shows your hosted usage limits. [Check where numbers are available](/getting-started/number-countries).
</Note>

<NoCodePrompt />

## Before you begin

You need:

- An email address and password.
- A mobile phone that can receive text messages, if LobbyStack asks you to verify one before your free trial number. LobbyStack can't verify internet (VoIP) numbers.
- A public website URL if you want LobbyStack to import website knowledge during onboarding.

## Onboarding steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to the LobbyStack sign-up page, enter your email address and password, and submit the form.

    LobbyStack opens onboarding after signup.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name your business">
    Enter the business name that LobbyStack should show across the dashboard and customer-facing details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import your website">
    Enter a public website URL to start a background website import, or choose **I don't have a website**.

    Website import crawls public pages such as services, about, FAQ, hours, and contact pages. Private pages, local network URLs, and login-only pages are not imported.

    LobbyStack shows the import's progress while it runs. Click **Skip the wait** to move on, and the import keeps going. It also sets your agent's caller language from your site. Without a website, you pick the language yourself.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your number">
    Pick your number's country, then a number from the list. Without a paid plan, it's free for 14 days. See [how the free trial works](/getting-started/claim-your-number#free-trial-number).

    LobbyStack asks you to verify a mobile number first when it can't confirm the network you're setting up from, or when a free trial already started from that network in the last 30 days. During the trial, calls transfer only to that mobile.

    If LobbyStack doesn't sell numbers in your country yet, ask for an email when it does, and keep testing your agent in your browser.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tell LobbyStack your goal">
    Pick what you want your agent to do first, then say how you heard about LobbyStack. You can skip both.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Meet your agent">
    Test your agent in your browser with a call or a chat, or call your number if you chose one. Allow microphone access when your browser asks.

    On the same screen, change your agent's name, voice, greeting, and caller language. After your first test that works, LobbyStack shows the plans once. Click **Not now** to keep testing.

    Click **Go to dashboard** when you're ready.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Forward your business line">
    To keep the number your customers already dial, [forward it to your LobbyStack number](/getting-started/forward-calls).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Before real callers reach your agent, open it from **Agents** and review its **General** page, instructions and rules. Then check **Details**, **Knowledge**, **Services** and **Employees** in **Business**. To add documents, open **Add more sources** in **Getting started** on **Home**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
