Call forwarding sends calls from your current business line to your LobbyStack number, and your receptionist answers them. Customers keep dialing the number they know. You turn forwarding on with your phone carrier, from the phone your customers call.

The dashboard shows these steps with your number filled in. During the free trial, click **Set up forwarding** on **Home**. After you subscribe, click **Call forwarding guide** on the page that confirms your plan.

## Choose what to forward

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Calls you miss** | Your phone rings first. When you don't answer, your receptionist picks up. |
| **All calls** | Your receptionist answers every call, and your phone doesn't ring. |

Calls you miss covers three cases: you don't answer, you're on another call, or your phone is off or has no signal. Some carriers handle all three with one code, and others use one code for each case.

## Before you start

- Find your LobbyStack number in **Settings** > **Phone number**.
- The codes on this page use the example number (555) 123-4567. Replace `5551234567` with the 10 digits of your LobbyStack number.
- Some carriers want a 1 in front of the number, so their codes show `15551234567`. Keep the 1 and replace the 10 digits after it.

## Turn forwarding on with a code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dial the code">
    On the phone your customers call, dial your carrier's code from the **Turn on** column. Some codes end without a number, such as `*72`. For those, the line asks for the number after you press Call: enter it in the format the table shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Call">
    A tone or a message confirms that forwarding is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dial the extra codes">
    For calls you miss, some carriers split the cases into separate codes. Dial the **Busy** code to also forward calls that come in while you're on the phone. Dial the **Unreachable** code to also forward calls when your phone is off or has no signal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To turn forwarding off later, dial the codes in the **Turn off** column.

## Turn forwarding on in settings

Some carriers and most business phone systems use a settings screen instead of a code.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Open the menu path from the **Turn on** column. Depending on the carrier, it's on your phone, in the carrier's app, or on its website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the forwarding number">
    Forward unanswered calls, or all calls, to your LobbyStack number, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To turn forwarding off later, go back to the same menu.

Call your business number from another phone. If you forward calls you miss, let it ring. Your receptionist should answer.

Ask your carrier to forward unanswered calls, or all your calls, to your LobbyStack number.
