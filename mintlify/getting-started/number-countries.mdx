LobbyStack sells phone numbers in the United States, Canada and Puerto Rico. In all three, you can start with a number free for 14 days or get one with a paid plan.

## Countries LobbyStack is adding

LobbyStack is adding the United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand. Australian numbers will come with paid plans only, without a free trial.

## Your number's country

During setup, you pick your number's country from the countries LobbyStack sells in. You need a number to finish setup, unless LobbyStack sells none in your country.

## Countries without numbers yet

If LobbyStack doesn't sell numbers in your country yet, pick **Other country** in the number step's **Country** list, then pick your country in the list that opens. Click **Email me when they're available** to get an email when LobbyStack starts selling them, then **Continue without a number**. Browser test calls still work, so you can set up and test your agent in the meantime.
