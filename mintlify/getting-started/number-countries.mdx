LobbyStack sells phone numbers in the United States and Canada. In both countries, you can start with a number free for 14 days or get one with a paid plan.

## Countries LobbyStack is adding

LobbyStack is adding the United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand. Australian numbers will come with paid plans only, without a free trial.

## Your number's country

During setup, you pick a country when you verify your mobile number. Your LobbyStack number comes from that country, and your mobile number has to be from it too.

## Countries without numbers yet

If LobbyStack doesn't sell numbers in your country yet, you can't get a number there. When you verify your mobile number, you can ask for an email when LobbyStack starts selling them. Browser test calls still work, so you can set up and test your receptionist in the meantime.
