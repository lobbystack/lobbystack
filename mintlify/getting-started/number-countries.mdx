LobbyStack sells phone numbers in the United States, Canada and Puerto Rico. In all three, you can start with a number free for 14 days or get one with a paid plan.

## Countries LobbyStack is adding

LobbyStack is adding the United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand. Australian numbers will come with paid plans only, without a free trial.

## Your number's country

During setup, you pick your number's country from the countries LobbyStack sells in. A free trial number needs no mobile number; LobbyStack asks for one only when it can't confirm the network you're setting up from, or when a free trial already started from that network in the last 30 days.

## Countries without numbers yet

If LobbyStack doesn't sell numbers in your country yet, you can't get a number there. On the number step, you can ask for an email when LobbyStack starts selling them. Browser test calls still work, so you can set up and test your agent in the meantime.
