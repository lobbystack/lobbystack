Your LobbyStack account gives you access to the dashboard and its business workspaces. This guide covers signup, login, email changes, and password changes.

## Creating an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-up page">
    Open the LobbyStack sign-up page. You can reach it from the homepage by clicking **Try for free**, or by navigating directly to the sign-up URL.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your email and password">
    Provide the email address you want to use for your account and choose a password.

    Use a strong, unique password. LobbyStack enforces a minimum password length and complexity requirement.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit the form">
    Click **Sign up**. LobbyStack creates the account and opens the business onboarding flow.

    See the [Get started guide](/quickstart) for a full walkthrough of onboarding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Logging in

Go to the LobbyStack login page and enter your email and password. If you forget your password, click **Forgot password**. LobbyStack emails you a code. Enter it with your new password.

## Creating another workspace

To set up another business, open the workspace switcher in the sidebar and click **Create workspace**. LobbyStack opens onboarding for the new workspace.

To leave onboarding before you finish, click **Back to dashboard** in the top right. LobbyStack returns you to the workspace you came from. The new workspace stays in the workspace switcher, and you can finish its setup later.

## Changing your account settings

After signing in, click **Settings** in the sidebar. Change your email address and password in **Profile and security**, under **Your account**. Change the business name in **Team**, under **Workspace**.

### Business name

Your business name appears in your dashboard and is used by the AI agent when it greets callers (unless you override the greeting with custom text).

1. In **Settings** > **Team**, click **Change** beside **Business name**.
2. Edit the name and click **Save**.

The change takes effect immediately.

### Email address

Changing your email address requires confirmation.

1. In **Settings** > **Profile and security**, find the **Email** field.
2. Enter your new email address and click **Save**.
3. LobbyStack sends a confirmation email to the new address. Open it and click the confirmation link.
4. Your email address is updated once you confirm.

<Note>
  Until you click the link in the confirmation email, your account continues to use your original email address. Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive within a few minutes.
</Note>

### Password

1. In **Settings** > **Profile and security**, find the **Password** section.
2. Enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password.
3. Click **Save**.

Your current session remains active after a password change. Review [account security](/settings/account) for the current account workflow.
