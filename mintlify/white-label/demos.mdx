A prospect demo is a receptionist built from a prospect's website, which the prospect can talk to on your address before they buy. Everyone on your team makes demos on the **Demos** page. When the prospect claims a demo, it becomes one of your businesses, with the prospect as its owner.

## Make a demo

You need your dashboard address before you make a demo, because the demo opens there. On **Demos**, click **Create demo**, then fill in the **Prepare a demo** form:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the prospect">
    Enter the **Business name** and the **Website URL** the receptionist learns from. Add a **Greeting** if you want a specific one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add suggested prompts">
    Under **Suggested prompts**, write at least two questions the prospect can ask, one per line, such as `Can I request a quote?`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose its access (owners and admins)">
    Under **Access once the prospect claims it**, pick **Full access** or **Activity only**. A member's demo gets full access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create demo**. LobbyStack reads the prospect's website to build the demo, and nobody can open it until you publish it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each demo's card shows its progress under **Ingestion:**, **Snapshot:** and **Prompts:**. The demo is ready to publish once all three are done.

## Publish and share the link

A demo opens from a secure link that you send the prospect. LobbyStack keeps no copy of the link, so the console shows it only in the browser session that created or rotated it:

1. Click **Publish** once the demo is ready.
2. Click **Open secure link** to open the demo, and copy its address to send to the prospect.

After you reload the page, the console no longer shows the link. Click **Rotate link** to get a new one, which stops the old one from working.

The link opens on your address, under your brand. The prospect starts a live voice call in the browser with a receptionist that already knows their business, and sees your suggested prompts under **Try asking:**. Booking and other full features stay off until they claim it. A demo expires 30 days after you create it.

## Turn off or delete a demo

Click **Revoke** to turn a demo off, so its link stops working. Owners and admins can then click **Delete** to erase a demo and its calls and chats, once it's turned off or expired and nobody claimed it.

## Count a demo's usage

A demo's calls and chats use your pool's minutes and chats, like any of your businesses. Each card shows its minutes and chats this month. A demo has no package and no phone number until someone claims it.

Click **Open** on a demo to work inside its dashboard, the way you open a business. The demo's audit log records the open.

## When the prospect claims the demo

The prospect clicks **Claim this receptionist** and signs in or creates an account on your address. They become the owner, and the business stays with your partner at the access level you chose:

- The demo moves from **Demos** to **Businesses**, where owners and admins can sell it a package.
- Settings your team changed during the demo stay. Settings nobody changed switch from the demo's instructions to a new business's defaults.
- The owner's onboarding starts where an owner you invited would start.
- Only you can release the business to its owner later, as with any of your businesses.

People on your team can't claim a demo themselves.
