When callers need to cancel or move an appointment, LobbyStack can handle the request during the call.

Instead of waiting for your team to read a message, check the calendar, and reply manually, the AI receptionist can identify the appointment, confirm the request, and update the booking for you.

## What the AI can handle

LobbyStack can help customers:

- Find their upcoming appointment.
- Cancel an appointment they no longer need.
- Reschedule to another available time.
- Confirm the change before it is applied.
- Complete the request during the call.

This is designed for everyday appointment changes that usually take your team away from higher-value work.

## Built-in confirmation before changes

Appointment changes are not made from a casual request alone. LobbyStack asks for enough detail to make sure the customer is changing the right appointment.

By default, the receptionist uses:

- The phone number from the call.
- The customer's name.
- An appointment detail, such as the appointment time or service.
- A clear final confirmation before canceling or rescheduling.

If the receptionist cannot confirm the appointment confidently, it will not make the change automatically. It can hand the conversation to your team instead.

<Note>
  LobbyStack helps you apply consistent confirmation steps, but it does not replace your own legal or privacy review. Configure the level of verification that fits your business, region, and appointment type.
</Note>

## Choose how callers are verified

For businesses that want extra assurance, **Caller verification** adds a check before the AI changes an appointment:

| Option | What the caller does |
| --- | --- |
| **Phone number and booking details** | Calls from the number the appointment was booked with and says its time or service. This is the default. |
| **Texted one-time code** | Also reads back a code the receptionist texts to that number. |
| **Keypad PIN** | Also types a PIN on the phone keypad. Your team sets each customer's PIN, and no text is needed. |

A stricter check is useful when appointments are sensitive, high-value, or subject to stricter internal policies.

### Set up keypad PINs

With **Keypad PIN** selected, the receptionist asks the caller to type their PIN on the phone keypad and press the pound key (#). The PIN never reaches the AI or the call transcript, and the receptionist won't accept a PIN that's said aloud.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    In the dashboard sidebar, go to **Contacts** and open the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the PIN">
    On the **Details** tab, under **Keypad PIN**, select **Set PIN**. Type 4 to 8 digits, or select **Generate**, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the PIN to the customer">
    LobbyStack stores the PIN securely and can't show it again. If the customer forgets it, set a new one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To give PINs to many customers at once, use the [command line](/api/cli) or the [API](/api/overview). `lobbystack contacts generate-pins --all` makes up a PIN for every contact that has a phone number and no PIN, and shows each PIN this one time. `lobbystack contacts set-pins` sets PINs you choose. In the API, these are `POST /contacts/passcodes/generate` and `POST /contacts/passcodes`.

A caller can try their PIN 3 times on each change. After 5 wrong PINs in a row, even across calls, the PIN is locked and the contact shows it as locked. Setting a new PIN unlocks it.

Callers without a PIN, or with a locked one, can't change appointments by phone. The receptionist passes their cancellation request to your team instead.

<Warning>
  On a self-hosted LobbyStack, PINs are checked with the server's `OTP_HASH_SECRET` (or `ENCRYPTION_KEY` when it isn't set). If that secret changes, every saved PIN stops working and has to be set again.
</Warning>

## Automatic calendar updates

When a change is approved, LobbyStack updates the appointment record and syncs the change to your connected calendar.

For cancellations, the appointment is marked as canceled and the calendar event is updated or removed according to the calendar connection.

For rescheduling, LobbyStack checks availability before offering a new time. If the time is available, the appointment is moved and the calendar sync is refreshed.

<Tip>
  Connect Google Calendar before relying on automatic rescheduling. This lets LobbyStack avoid offering times that are already busy.
</Tip>

## Turn appointment changes on or off

You control whether the receptionist can cancel or reschedule, and how it verifies callers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI settings">
    In the dashboard sidebar, go to **Agent** > **AI settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find appointment changes">
    Scroll to **Appointment changes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what the AI can do">
    Turn cancellation and rescheduling on or off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose verification strength">
    Pick an option in **Caller verification** if you want stricter verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended setup

For the best customer experience:

- Add clear service entries so customers can describe what they booked.
- Connect Google Calendar so LobbyStack can check availability and sync changes.
- Keep your transfer number up to date for cases that need human review.
- Use a texted one-time code or a keypad PIN for sensitive or high-value appointments.
