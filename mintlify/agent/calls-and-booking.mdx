Each agent's **General** page decides where it sends callers who need a person and how it handles appointments. Click **Agents** in the sidebar and open the agent. It opens on **General**, and these settings follow **Name, greeting and voice**.

Each agent keeps its own settings here, so an after-hours agent can book differently from your daytime one.

## Default transfer number

Under **Transfers**, the **Default transfer number** is the phone number your agent calls when it hands a caller to a person. When a caller asks for an [employee](/settings/employees) who has a phone number, your agent transfers the call to that employee instead.

Use a number that someone answers during business hours. If different teams handle different requests, keep the default number as the safest general handoff and add [rules](/agent/rules) for when a caller should be transferred.

<Note>
  Transfer numbers must be in a country listed in the country menu next to the field: the United States, Canada, Puerto Rico, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, Portugal, Mexico, Brazil, Israel, India, Japan, Australia, New Zealand, and Serbia. Transfers to numbers elsewhere don't connect.
</Note>

If your free trial started with a verified mobile number, your agent transfers calls only to it during the trial, and you can't save a different number. Upgrade to Starter or Pro to change it.

LobbyStack also forwards calls to this number when your voice minutes run out or overages reach your spending cap. Without one, those calls go to the mobile number you verified, if you have one. See [Usage and limits](/billing/usage).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter a number">
    Enter the complete phone number in **Default transfer number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** beside the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add rules if needed">
    In the agent's **Rules**, describe when it should transfer, take a message, or ask for callback details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Add a transfer number when you want live handoff to a person. Without one, the agent takes callback or follow-up details so your team can respond from the dashboard.
</Note>

## Staff answers first

**Staff answers first** rings one staff phone before your agent answers. You press 1 to take the call. If nobody presses 1 before the ring time ends, your agent answers as usual. A voicemail greeting can't take the call, because it never presses 1.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick when to ring">
    Under **Staff answers first**, set **Ring your team first** to **During business hours** or **Always**. **During business hours** follows your [opening hours](/settings/business-details#opening-hours) and skips closures.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the staff phone">
    Enter the number in **Staff phone**. It starts with your default transfer number, and you can change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the ring time">
    Choose 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 or 60 seconds in **Ring for**. The default is 15.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add messages if you want them">
    **Message before ringing** plays before the staff phone rings. **Message before the agent answers** plays when nobody takes the call. Each holds 200 characters, and callers hear them in your default customer language. Serbian has no voice for these messages yet, so a business in Serbian skips them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save staff answers first** at the bottom of the section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each ring uses one [transfer attempt](/billing/usage). Calls you answer use no voice minutes and appear in the [Inbox](/dashboard/inbox) as **Answered by your team**.

Calls you answer end after 30 minutes. After 600 minutes of answered calls in 24 hours, new calls go straight to your agent until that total drops. This limit applies on every plan.

<Warning>
  Don't use a phone that forwards unanswered calls to your LobbyStack number. The call comes straight back to your agent.
</Warning>

Staff answers first needs a paid plan and works on Twilio numbers. On other numbers, the setting shows as unavailable. To compare it with carrier forwarding, see [Forward your business line to LobbyStack](/getting-started/forward-calls#choose-what-to-forward).

## Booking

Under **Booking**, **Appointments** sets what the agent does when a caller wants an appointment:

- **Books appointments**: the agent checks your open times and books the appointment
- **Takes requests**: the agent notes the service and preferred time, and your team confirms it from the Inbox
- **Doesn't book**: the agent takes a message instead

Before you rely on booking, add your [services](/settings/services) and [opening hours](/settings/business-details#opening-hours). [Connect your calendar](/integrations/overview) so the agent avoids times already booked there.

## Appointment changes

Use **Appointment changes** to choose whether the agent can cancel or reschedule appointments, and how it checks who the caller is. You can require a texted one-time code or a keypad PIN before the agent changes any appointment.
