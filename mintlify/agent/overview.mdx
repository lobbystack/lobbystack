The **Agent** section controls how your AI receptionist behaves. It is split into AI settings, Knowledge, Services, Employees, and Rules.

Changes apply to new calls and website chat responses. They do not rewrite calls or messages that already happened.

## How configuration is used

LobbyStack prepares the saved Agent settings for each live call, so callers get stable behavior for the duration of the conversation.

For website chat, LobbyStack uses the current business context and saved conversation state when generating replies.

## Minimum setup for real callers

Before forwarding production calls, check that:

- Your greeting names your business and sets the right expectation.
- The default customer language is the one most of your callers speak, because calls open in it.
- A transfer number is saved if you want live human handoff. It must be in a [country LobbyStack can call](/agent/ai-settings/overview#default-transfer-number).
- Your knowledge entries include hours, location, services, pricing, policies, and common caller questions.
- Your services, employees, and Google Calendar connection are ready before you rely on appointment booking or appointment changes.
