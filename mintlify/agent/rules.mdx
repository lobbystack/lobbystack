The **Rules** page lets you shape your agent's behavior in plain text. Instead of building workflows or maintaining decision trees, write the instruction the way you would explain it to a teammate.

Rules are useful for actions, policies, and judgment calls: when to transfer a caller, when to take a callback message, what to do after hours, and how to handle questions that need a human response.

## What to put in rules

Good rules are specific and action-oriented:

- "If a caller says there is an emergency, transfer to the default transfer number."
- "If the caller asks for a human and transfer is unavailable, take a callback message with their name, phone number, and preferred callback time."
- "If the agent is not sure of an answer, say that the team will follow up instead of guessing."
- "For after-hours callers, take a message and include the caller's preferred callback window."

<Note>
  Rules let you define agent behavior in clear language. Keep each rule updated as your process changes.
</Note>

## Rule templates

| Scenario | Template |
| --- | --- |
| Live transfer | If a caller asks for a human or describes an urgent issue, transfer to the default transfer number. |
| Callback | If the caller wants a callback, collect their name, phone number, reason for calling, and preferred callback window. |
| Uncertain answer | If the agent is not confident, take a message for the team instead of guessing. |
| After-hours | Outside business hours, take a message and tell the caller the team will follow up during the next business day. |
| Pricing questions | If pricing depends on the customer's situation, explain the starting point and offer to have the team follow up with details. |

## Add a rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rules">
    Click **Agents** in the sidebar, open the agent, and click **Rules** in its menu. Each agent has its own rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a rule">
    Click **Add Rule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the instruction">
    Add a short title and the full instruction in the content field. To keep the rule to one channel, choose it under **Channel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add">
    Click **Add**. The rule appears at the bottom of the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep a rule to one channel

A rule applies on every channel until you choose one. Pick it under **Channel** when you add or edit the rule:

| Channel | Where the agent follows the rule |
| --- | --- |
| **All channels** | Calls, texts, website chat and Instagram |
| **Calls** | Phone calls and calls from your browser |
| **Text messages** | Replies to texts |
| **Website chat** | The chat widget on your website |
| **Instagram** | Instagram direct messages |

The **Channel** column shows where each rule applies. Use a channel rule for how the agent talks there, such as "Keep replies under 300 characters" for texts.

<Note>
  Rules replaced the **Instructions** page. Each agent's instructions became a rule for their channel, titled in your business's language, such as **On calls** or **In texts**. Chat instructions became a **Website chat** rule, and an **Instagram** rule too if your business had Instagram connected.
</Note>

## Order your rules

Your agent reads rules in priority order, starting at the top of the table. Put the rules that matter most first.

To move a rule, drag it by its handle. You can also open **More options** on the rule and choose **Move up** or **Move down**.

## Transfers and callbacks

Transfers use the [default transfer number](/agent/calls-and-booking#default-transfer-number) on the agent's **General** page, or the number of an [employee](/settings/employees) the caller asks for. Transfer numbers must be in a [country LobbyStack can call](/agent/calls-and-booking#default-transfer-number), and during a free trial that started with a verified mobile number, calls transfer only to that mobile. When a live handoff is not the right next step, the agent can create a follow-up task with callback details. Those tasks appear in the dashboard and on call detail pages.

## Recording and transcripts

LobbyStack stores call transcripts and can store call recordings. Operators review recordings in the [Inbox](/dashboard/inbox) when a recording is available.

Use rules for call-handling behavior. Manage recording and retention policy in your workspace and compliance settings.
