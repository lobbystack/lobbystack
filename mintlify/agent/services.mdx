The **Services** page lists what callers can book. Each service has a title, a duration, an optional price, a description, and a **Bookable** switch. Your receptionist uses them to explain what you offer, quote prices, and book appointments of the right length.

## Add a service

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Services">
    Go to **Agent** > **Services**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add service">
    Click **Add Service**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the service and set its length">
    Enter a clear **Title**, such as "Initial Consultation", and pick the **Duration**. Your receptionist uses the duration to find open times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a price">
    Choose a price type and enter the amount, or leave the amount empty. See [Prices](#prices).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the service">
    In **Content**, describe what the service includes and who it's for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add">
    Leave **Bookable** on so your receptionist offers the service on calls, then click **Add**. The service appears in the Services table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change a service, click its row, edit it, and click **Save**.

## Prices

| Price type | What you enter | What your receptionist tells callers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fixed** | One amount | That price. |
| **From** | The lowest price | A starting price, such as "from $40". |
| **Between** | The lowest and the highest price | The range. |
| **In person** | No amount | The business sets the price once it knows what the caller needs. |

Your receptionist quotes the price you entered. If you leave the amount empty, it doesn't guess one, and the **Price** column shows **Not set**. Prices use the currency you pick in [AI settings](/agent/ai-settings/overview#currency).

## Duplicate, disable, or delete a service

Open the **More options** menu on a service's row:

- **Duplicate** copies the service and adds "(copy)" to its title. Edit the copy to set up a similar service.
- **Disable** stops your receptionist from offering the service and keeps it in the table. Click **Enable** to offer it again.
- **Delete** removes the service. Your receptionist stops offering it, and past appointments still show it.

## Choose who does each service

If you add [employees](/agent/employees), choose the services each one does on the **Employees** page. Callers can then book a specific person for a service.

## Help callers book the right service

Write service entries the way callers ask for them:

- Use the plain customer-facing service name.
- Add eligibility and prep instructions to the description only when those details are stable.
- Mention other names callers use for the service in the description.
- Keep unavailable or seasonal services disabled.

## Service examples

| Service | Helpful details to include |
| --- | --- |
| Initial consultation | Who it is for, typical duration, whether it can be booked online, and what the caller should prepare. |
| Emergency repair | What counts as urgent, whether same-day service is available, and when the receptionist should transfer the caller. |
| Follow-up appointment | Who should book it, how long it usually takes, and whether it must be with a specific team member. |
| Seasonal service | The months it is offered, any booking window, and what callers should know before requesting it. |
