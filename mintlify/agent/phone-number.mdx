Your phone number is one of an agent's channels. Starter and Pro include one number. Without a paid plan, your number is a [14-day free trial](/getting-started/claim-your-number#free-trial-number).

## Find your number

Click **Agents** in the sidebar, open the agent, and click **Channels** in its menu. The number shows under **Phone number**. Click **Manage** beside it to open the number's page.

Configure what callers hear and where the agent transfers them on its **General** page, under [Name, greeting and voice](/agent/greeting-and-voice) and [Transfers](/agent/calls-and-booking#default-transfer-number).

## Get a number

If you don't have a number yet, open the default agent's **Channels** and click **Get a number**. On Free, click **Choose my number** to try one free for 14 days. To keep your current business line, [forward it to your LobbyStack number](/getting-started/forward-calls).

The [default agent](/agent/manage-agents#the-default-agent) gets your first number. To have another agent answer it instead, [move the number](/agent/channels#move-a-channel-to-another-agent) to that agent.

## Buy a number for another agent

On Starter or Pro, another agent can have a number of its own. Each extra number costs what our carrier charges us for a number in its country, with no markup, and goes on your plan's bill. An extra number in the US or Canada costs $1.15 a month. Enterprise has no number limit, so its extra numbers are free.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent's channels">
    Open the agent and click **Channels** in its menu. Under **Phone number**, click **Buy a number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a number">
    Choose a country and area code. The monthly price for that country shows above the numbers. Click **Buy** beside the number you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the price">
    Click **Buy number** to confirm. The number answers as that agent once it's ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your workspace needs its first number before you can buy another. You pay for an extra number each month until you release it. If your oldest number goes, the next one becomes your included number and stops costing extra.

## Replace a number

Eligible workspaces can replace their number once:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the number">
    On the agent's **Channels**, click **Manage** beside the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for a number">
    Choose a country and area code, then review the available local numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the replacement">
    Select a number and confirm the change. Your previous number stays active for 30 days. Update your [call forwarding](/getting-started/forward-calls), website links, and printed material that use the previous number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Number replacement affects live callers. Confirm the new number before publishing it or changing external forwarding rules.
</Warning>

When your paid plan ends, you keep your numbers for 30 more days, then LobbyStack releases them. Extra numbers stop costing anything when the plan ends. Review [plans and billing](/billing/plans) before changing plans.
