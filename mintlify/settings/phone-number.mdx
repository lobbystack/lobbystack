The **Settings** > **Phone number** page shows the number callers use to reach your receptionist. Starter and Pro include one number. Without a paid plan, your number is a [14-day free trial](/getting-started/claim-your-number#free-trial-number).

## Review the active number

Use this page to confirm the number assigned to the active business. Configure the greeting, transfer destination, and caller behavior under [AI settings](/agent/ai-settings/overview).

## Replace a number

Eligible workspaces can replace their number once:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Phone number">
    Go to **Settings** > **Phone number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for a number">
    Choose a country and area code, then review the available local numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the replacement">
    Select a number and confirm the change. Update any forwarding rules, website links, and printed material that use the previous number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Number replacement affects live callers. Confirm the new number before publishing it or changing external forwarding rules.
</Warning>

When your paid plan ends, you keep your number for 30 more days, then LobbyStack releases it. Review [plans and billing](/billing/plans) before changing plans.
