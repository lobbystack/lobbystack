Click **Settings** in the dashboard sidebar. The sidebar switches to the settings menu, with two groups: **Workspace** for settings every agent and teammate shares, and **Your account** for your own. The row at the top takes you back to the main menu.

Each agent's own settings, such as its greeting, rules and channels, live on the agent instead. See [Configure your AI agent](/agent/overview).

## Workspace

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Business details** | [Business summary, opening hours, customer and team languages, time zone and currency](/settings/business-details) |
| **Knowledge** | [Text entries, documents, website imports and unanswered questions](/settings/knowledge) |
| **Services** | [What callers can book, with duration and price](/settings/services) |
| **Employees** | [The people callers can book or ask for](/settings/employees) |
| **Team** | [The business name, member invitations, roles and access](/settings/team) |
| **Usage** | [Usage this billing period, what's left and when it resets](/billing/usage) |
| **Plan and billing** | [Your plan, upgrades, subscription and spending cap](/billing/plans) |
| **Connections** | [Google Calendar, Cal.com and Calendly](/integrations/overview) |
| **Webhooks** | [Events sent to your own tools](/integrations/webhooks) |
| **API keys** | [Keys for the API and connected AI assistants](/api/authentication) |

A business that a partner runs shows **Billing** instead of **Plan and billing**, and **Partner access**, which lists when the partner's team opened the business.

## Your account

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile and security** | [Your email address and password](/settings/account) |
| **Language and theme** | Dashboard language, time format and whether the dashboard shares telemetry data |
| **Notifications** | [The email and SMS alerts you get](/settings/notifications) |

## What you see

Settings shows only the pages your role and your business's access level let you open. For example, only owners and admins see **API keys**. Links to old settings pages, such as `/settings/account`, still open the page that replaced them.
