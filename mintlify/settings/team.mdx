The **Settings** > **Team** page controls the active business name and workspace access. Owners and administrators can invite and remove members.

## Change the business name

Choose **Change** beside the business name, enter the new name, and save it. LobbyStack uses this name across the dashboard and customer-facing details.

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team">
    Go to **Settings** > **Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the member details">
    Click **Invite**, enter the teammate's email address, and choose a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Submit the form. LobbyStack emails a time-limited invitation to that address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The recipient must sign in with the invited email address before accepting the invitation.

## Choose a role

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full workspace access, including team administration. |
| **Admin** | Manages members, workspace settings, and operations. |
| **Viewer** | Views operational dashboard data without team administration. |

## Revoke or remove access

Cancel a pending invitation when the recipient no longer needs access. Remove an existing member to end their access to the business. Workspace owners cannot be removed from this page.
